# Vraki West March Campaign D&D

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Deovandski/HusbandOutWorld/main/WorldMapMD.png)

## SPOILER WARNING!

This repo is a West Marches style D&D 5e campaign set in the world of the #HusbandOutWorld series. The World Lore summary and the DM files reveal secrets of the world, including some from unreleased story notes.

## 

### What is this?

A complete, self-contained campaign: a summary of the world's lore, rules for running an open-table West March, travel maps, quests, encounters, stat blocks and pre-generated adventurers. You don't need anything from the main #HusbandOutWorld repo to play.

The campaign takes place **in the world** of the light novels, but it is **not tied to their story**. The famous characters exist and might show up as NPCs or rumors, but the West March has its own adventures, set by default in the 1040s A.G.

### What is a West Marches campaign?

An open table! Instead of one fixed party, a pool of players schedule their own sessions, form whatever party shows up that day, and head out from a shared home base into a dangerous, persistent world. Whatever one party discovers, maps, kills, or pisses off stays that way for everyone else.

The Four Kingdoms were practically built for it: Forway's embassies already run a quest board, a ranking system from Pearl to Sapphire, revival services (for a fee, of course), and a decentralized army of adventurers deep in debt. Pick up a quest, sign the contract in blood, and try not to come back as Walking Flesh.

### Travel without math

No distances, no Egua speeds, no load percentages. The travel map has circles drawn along the routes connecting places; **each circle represents 1, 2 or 3 days of travel — the DM chooses**, to make a journey shorter or longer.
