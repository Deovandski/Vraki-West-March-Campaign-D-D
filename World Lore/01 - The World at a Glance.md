# The World at a Glance

## Elevator pitch
A high-magic, **godless** world where magic is treated as science. Four kingdoms that spent centuries killing each other were overthrown by their own court scientists, who replaced war with paperwork: one currency, one calendar, one set of units, and a neutral city, **Forway**, that runs embassies in every kingdom. Adventuring is a **Forway government job**. You take quests off the embassy board, sign contracts in blood, and if you die, Forway will revive you... and send you the bill.

## What makes Vraki different
- **No gods, no religion-driven plots, no good vs. evil.** People revere ancestors, nature, the sea, a giant tree, or money. The "evil" is greed, debt, and old experiments that never stopped running.
- **No elves or dwarves.** Peoples are humans (mostly mixed-blood), the giant tusked **Darfaries**, the small **Wozefaries**, the flying child-like **Sorefaries**, mute crocodile-folk **Cruros**, and a handful of ancient races that are extinct, hidden, or watching from far away.
- **Magic = Soul Energy.** Everyone has a soul that stores energy. You cast through an *interface* (most people use **sigil rings**), power it with your own soul or with **nari stones** (soul batteries), and you never shout spell names.
- **Nari gels** let anyone change their body: breathe water, grow wings, smell like a Wozen, look like someone else. They're as common as coffee and as regulated as drugs.
- **Death is a setback, not an ending** — as long as someone finds your body in time and you can pay.
- **Contracts rule everything.** Slavery is illegal, but selling your freedom by contract is legal. Read the fine print.
- **Food matters.** Every city has dishes, prices, and festivals. Taverns are second homes.
- **Tone:** adult, irreverent, crude humor, grounded "science", bureaucratic, sometimes grim. Expect brothels, debt, drunk kings, and bandits named "Beer Balls".

## The map in one breath
In the south of the planet: the **desert** in the west (Gandew's lone mountain, Sundeath, Flatland oasis), **Forway** in the middle, the **Buhraies Plains** (a 2 km-high plateau of pink killer trees) to the north-east with **Tolerideme** on top, **Sanbi** on the east coast, the **Freeish** forests to the south-west with **Blood** and **Sweetlake**, islands like **Triangle** and **Endlessfog** out at sea, and **Hifuna**, an irradiated wasteland, walling off the north.

## Who's who in one breath
- **Forway** — the neutral bureaucratic center; runs embassies, adventurers, taxes, currency.
- **Sanbi** — friendly, handsy, frugal fishing kingdom ruled by four "power players".
- **Tolerideme** — fat, rich, punctual, honest brewers and farmers who never haggle.
- **Gandew** — Darfaries-only mountain kingdom where every dispute ends in a duel.
- **Freeish** — loose federation of forest villages that hate each other a little.
- **Bandits & factions** — from road robbers to secret societies of the rich.
- **Ancient races** — the Buhraies made most of the world's monsters (and humans); their castle **Bahyum** still churns out undead.

## Playing here
Your character is a badge-carrying adventurer working out of an **embassy**. You rise through **Pearl → Amethyst → Aquamarine → Sapphire**. You get paid in **balls** (marble-sized gems). You probably owe someone money. Welcome.
