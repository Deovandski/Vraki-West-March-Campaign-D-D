# Money, Prices & Economy

## Balls (the common currency)
Introduced by the Prosperity Treaty (Section 3). **Bartering between treaty citizens is illegal**; haggling is fine if the seller agrees (Toleridemians never agree). Coins are marble-sized gemstone **balls** (~2 cm) wrapped in golden metal filigree that protects the stone and stops counterfeits. Each kingdom mints under embassy oversight; Gandew mints Sapphires and Aquamarines, Flatland mines Amethysts.

| Ball | Worth | What it buys |
|---|---|---|
| **Pearl** | base unit | Food and beer, a hostel bed, a wagon ride, used clothes, paper and ink, cheap gear, weak nari gels |
| **Amethyst** | 50 pearls | A nari stone, household goods, average gear, decent lodging with breakfast, maps, most nari gels, sigil rings, a tamed Winga, a badge |
| **Aquamarine** | 10 amethysts = 500 pearls | Custom/good gear, a young tamed Egua, a tamed Big Winga, guarded travel, fancy hotels, fundamental school, some people's life savings |
| **Sapphire** | 10 aquamarines = 5,000 pearls | An adult tamed Egua, property, founding a guild, lifelong contracts, specialized school |

Pearls are harvested by Cruros in Sanbi's swamps; the gems are mined ~1 km deep in the mountains between Flatland and Blood.

**What a day looks like:** a Gyut berry picker earns about **1 pearl per kg** (30–60 minutes of work). A newcomer working a 12-hour day might spend 5 pearls on dinner and cheap beer, 5 on a hostel bed, and save 2. **Carbey** grass sets the base value of a pearl.

## Distance cost tiers
The farther goods travel, the more they cost. Goods are grouped by how much distance raises their price:
- **Tier I (cheap):** natural resources, fresh kills (kept with ice spells), paper.
- **Tier II (common):** nari stones, gems, black honey, sigil rings, low-quality gear, inks, processed meat, large furs.
- **Tier III (expensive):** gold, titanium, good gear, nari gels, jerky, small/quality furs, live animals, rare parts.
- **Tier IV (nobility):** custom gear, master-crafted household items, top hides, banned nari gels.

## Trade regions
The treaty bans mass production of goods unless a region holds a **comparative advantage** granted by Forway (renewed every 5 years, with price ceilings and quotas). Forway sits in every region.
- **Freeish region** (Freeish, Blood, Sweetlake, Deltacross, Triangle, Sundeath): flora and fauna.
- **Gandew region** (Gandew, Flatland, Sundeath): minerals, Wooly products, chitin.
- **Sanbi region** (Sanbi, Waterfall, Triangle, Tastyleaf, Endlessfog): Sanbi fish, fish meal, fish oil, salt.
- **Tolerideme region** (Tolerideme, Waterfall, Tastyleaf, Wingarest): Domar Carbey, Wood People goods, Wingas, booze.

## Taxes
Forway cities pay 20% of their wealth, villages 15%, kingdoms 10%. A city or village whose debt to Forway reaches 50% of its estimated output gets a Forway battalion and new leaders.

## Banking
Any embassy banks for you. Withdrawals and deposits take a few days while the embassy confirms with Forway. Loans are available (rates differ for individuals and guilds). Embassy **storage** delivers to any other embassy — slow, but the safest place to keep your stuff.
