# Kingdoms

Four kingdoms signed the Prosperity Treaty. Each keeps its own laws and customs as long as it obeys the treaty, and each hosts a Forway embassy.

---

## Sanbi (Sanbian) — on the east coast
*Friendly, handsy, frugal fishers on the east coast. ~45,000 people.*

**Government.** Power is split among four **power players** who meet every Freeday; any three can expel the fourth:
- **King** — foreign affairs and trade (King **Itagiba**, approachable, loves a party).
- **Queen** — local and social affairs, education, urban planning (Queen **Neferteri**, ambitious schemer).
- **Courtroom Scientist** — security, investigations, the guards.
- **King-Maid** — economy, bookkeeping, the castle maids. Frugality is law.
Royals have **blue strands of hair**. All royals belong to the **Sanbi Royal** guild.

**People.** Live-in-the-present, physically affectionate, direct, frugal, a bit sloppy but street-smart. Few festivals because every weekend is a party. Strangers may offer you a couch if you overdrink. Names are unique, Brazilian-Indigenous or Indian-sounding, and **can be changed freely** (the royals subsidize badge remakes). Gesture for "you're done for": flat hands clapping three times like a Sanbi fish mouth.

**Religion.** They pray to the sea every day ("our soul is tied to the sea") and honor the legend of the **True Sanbi King**, a giant fish that made a pact with one fishing family.

**Law.** Forway Law. Minor crimes are repaid by replacement; major criminals are **thrown into the sea** (survive and you're absolved).

**Guards.** Flexible fighters with knives, big bows (often with nari-gel-poisoned arrows), nari gels and nari bombs. "Everything is a weapon."

**Post.** Kingdom courier for 1 pearl flat, white envelopes, no Wingas (slow).

**Places.** Sanbi Castle (three floors plus three five-story towers: west = Soul Energy department and labs, south = royal quarters, east = guest rooms and a playroom; only one gate, by the **Sanbi Fish Fountain**), the Dockyard, the **Old Wharf** (burials), Ungandy's factory (deep-blue nari bombs), Main Street Market, the Fish Drier and Fish Oil factories, **The Dead Wozen** tavern, Miirin's Cookery (tapas and killer desserts), **Broken Beak Hotel** (adventurers from Tastyleaf), the **Shaved Bagarisa** tavern, the Fish Gut Triage suburbs. Two-to-three-story buildings with white lime interiors.

**Food.** Fried Fish Balls (5 pearls/stick of 10), Sanbi Beer (with fish oil; supposedly an aphrodisiac; ~6/L), Bread Beer (~3/L), Hard Mud snack.

**Festivals.** Sanbi Fish Parade (Adrian, everyone in fish costumes), the daily Safe Fishing Prayer dance at the docks, White Roundies Arrival (winter).

**Burial.** *Soul Send Away*: a collar of sea-floor stones, a weight, and into the sea from the Old Wharf: "A person lives from the sea, and so shall the sea live from the body."

**Marriage.** Shout your love at the Sanbi Fish Fountain; a kiss seals it.

**Economy.** Dried fish, Sanbi-scale clothing, fish meal and fish oil, salt, some fost stones, trade with Cruros.

**Local quests.** Crocus control, Cruro reconnaissance, Aldemeri bounties, Newland exploration, sea patrol.

**Threats.** Cruros if they grow too many; the True Sanbi King fish if the pact breaks.

## Tolerideme (Toleridemian) — atop the Buhraies Plains
*Fat, rich, punctual, honest brewers and farmers. ~70,000 people.*

**Government.** Two kings: the **Farm King** ("Dirt King"; carries an heirloom wooden shovel; currently **Alford Dirt-Lover**) and the **Alcohol King** ("Booze King"; carries an heirloom corkscrew; currently **Jimmie Whoop-Ass**), checked by a Forway-appointed courtroom scientist. A new king is chosen at an elite castle party.

**People.** They revere the **Three Smart Ladies** (Evedyn, Everyn and Evelyn), who raised the kingdom from nothing with soul energy farming and fish-meal-plus-poop fertilizer. Being fat is a status symbol; hats show profession and wealth. Honest, blunt, loud, **never haggle** (the stated price is the fair price), do exactly what the contract says, **punctual to the minute**, and use soul energy instead of muscle whenever possible. Communal toilets (it's all fertilizer). Hours-long toasting rituals where a toastmaster raises glasses with magic. **No marriage**: adults do as they please (orgies aren't rare), but parents must raise any child together by law. Names are English and always have an **even number of letters**, usually 4 or 6.

**Law.** Forway Law, but punishment is **starvation**.

**Guards.** The most relaxed guards around — but in big packs, with gauntlets and slow, heavy elemental spells that break your will. Nari gels in a pinch.

**Places.** The Castle, the Smart Ladies statue, the Voluptuous Sex House, the Alcohol School, the Redtree Processing Center (Wood People goods), the Alcohol District. Uniform ~5-story buildings made of Wood People wood. Fourteen vassal villages (Tobame, Tocame, Todame… Totame) and Broken Redtree, walled with fost-stone-paralyzed Wood People.

**Food.** Domar Carbey in everything, fermented and long-storage eggs, Purply Smart Lambic Beer, Hearty Cocona Stew. Free school meals.

**Festivals.** **Shake Belly** (last days of Adrian, 3 days: shake your belly at a door marked with a ladle to earn food), **Harvest Festival** (Moacir; popular-vote booze contest).

**Burial.** Cremated with Wood People wood; ashes fertilize the family's land.

**Economy.** Domar Carbey, beer, ales and spirits, fost stones, Wood People goods, Wingas.

**Local quests.** Wood People control, **Bahyum control**, elevator maintenance (Waterfall), **Hifuna reconnaissance**, fost stone hauling.

## Gandew (Gandewian) — a lone 6 km mountain in the western desert
*Darfaries-only kingdom of duels and cave homes. ~43,000 people.*

**Government.** The king is whoever wins the **King Fest** (every Asa) by killing the old king and ripping out both his tusks. The loser's daughter must be given in marriage; sons can't challenge their father; there is no queen-regnant. The king is also the courtroom scientist unless he appoints one. Currently **King Nooru** and **Queen Satomi**.

**People.** Only Darfaries may live here; visitors are welcome. Private people who rarely make friends — but friends for life. They avoid soul energy when hands will do (hence great preserved foods). Communal and very clean. Shoes off indoors. Drink from two-handed bowls. **Mountain Greeting:** hands up, touch noses, stare, shake bellies. Names are Chinese/Japanese-sounding; your badge must say **High-, Medium- (Mid-) or Low-Gandew**.
**Blood Book:** families review each other every spring and villages every fall — promises read out, failures confessed, duels challenged, everything signed in blood, then a feast.

**Religion.** The ancient Darfaries who cleansed the mountain; spirits get lost in the desert without proper rites.

**Law.** Everything by duel (see Law). **Goodbye Cliff:** a ballista that launches Wozefaries and Sorefaries babies — and troublemakers — toward Flatland.

**Guards.** Heavy chitin armor, blunt weapons, often alone, helmets that lock their tusks shut (silent servitude).

**Places.** Tower Training (every juvenile trains a year), Central Farm, Central Herd, the Grand Hall (royal family), the **Low Pit** (hot sand), **Medium Pit**, **High Pit** (snow) — losers clean the pit — the Goodbye Cliff, Randy's Payupfront bars. Homes in caves and mud; fost stones inside, fire in public by tradition. Below the sand: beast tunnels where Darfaries hold "dungeon parties" with a bard serving drinks.

**Food.** Wooly cheeses (Gyut, Rakuja, artisan), Wooly sauce, Lollish-Floaters (~9), fried or stewed "Gandew Roundies" (Wooly testicles, 45–50), potted Wooly, Hard Mud, Milk String (frozen milk ribbons made with ice spells).

**Festivals.** **Weapon Fest** (Adrian), **King Fest** (Asa). **Burial:** cremation; bones washed in mountain sand and kept in an urn.

**Marriage.** Two Darfaries who like each other duel; a **draw** means they mate for life.

**Economy.** Wooly milk, wool and jerky, Rakuja, minerals, blacksmithing, **minting Sapphires and Aquamarines**.

**Local quests.** Desert road clearing, mining-hole maintenance, west and north exploration. Travel to Sundeath goes overland — tunnels are full of beasts.

## Freeish (Freeishan) — in the south-west forests
*Loose federation of forest villages. ~70,000 people across the villages.*

**Government.** Each village elects its leader (the leader's children can't run; in practice the four strongest guilds trade the post). Leaders elect a **Governor** who smooths things between villages (Deltacross votes through the back door). Leaders wear a **green circle tattoo** on the right hand; the governor's face is tattooed on the right side. The Governor State is a private 13 km² island.

**People.** Two village groups: the **Soil Group** (inner villages; farming and fishing; protective of Trunk People) and the **Blood Group** (outer villages; hunting; fighters; dislike Trunk People). They're warm to their own and think their village is best — and lately they've started fighting. Sprawling, flat towns where you need an Egua (or 20,000 steps a day); Trunk People light the roads from old soul-wood trees; giant scented flowers everywhere. Names sound Brazilian; people go by their village. "Meet the darkness" means to die. **FoodShare** couriers carry meals between villages.

**Law.** Set by each village; some follow Forway Law, some change it every election.

**Guards.** "Dollar-store Forway guards" in bone-wood armor — but brave, with short swords hidden in forearm sleeves, nari bombs to close distance, and reflexes trained on hallucinogens since childhood.

**Villages.** Governor State, Carbeyland (two "originals"), Farplace, Treetop, The Mill, Big Gyut, Sweet Siguash, Peaceful Life, Endland, Crossroads, Lishies Eater (oldest).

**Food.** Grilled Fihi with Rakuja sauce (5), Cadioca with fried fish and Domar Gyut (~14), Freeish salad bowl (9), Basi pulp (~5), roasted greenbread beans (5), Culuim street food (1–10), Nicabread sauce, Gyut Wrap (7). White clothes with colored embroidery.

**Festivals.** **Blood & Soil** (fall; the groups exchange gifts and party), **White Roundies Arrival** (winter; play fetch, trade food for whatever they bring up from the sea), the banned Village Competition (ended in a brawl in 998 A.G.).

**Marriage.** Propose with a bundle of fruit in the village plaza; married in front of the village leader; locals get a free guild.

**Burial.** In fields or woods, under a stone carved in Vraki.

**Guilds.** Gyut Farming, Greenbread Farming, Two Hearts (adventurers).

**Local quests.** Ganyrami bounty hunting, nari stone transport.
