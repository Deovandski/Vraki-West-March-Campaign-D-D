# Law, Treaties & Embassies

## The Prosperity Treaty (680 A.G.)
Written by **Freeish Paulin**'s rebellion and three courtroom scientists — **Asa the Powerful** (Gandew), **Adrian from North Tolerideme** (Tolerideme) and **Bijura Moacir** (Sanbi) — after they overthrew their own kings to end the Greed Period. Based on Sanbi law.

1. **Citizenship** is proven by a badge from an embassy. Freeish (built on old Ganyrami land) is an equal kingdom.
2. **Free movement** between kingdoms and cities. **Adventuring is a Forway job**, organized through embassies as a decentralized army. *Sub-section 3:* adventurers may **not** punish anyone unless a Forway guard or kingdom representative authorizes it.
3. **Trade** only in balls (no barter); the Vraki calendar and units are standard.
4. **Contracts** are binding when both sides get roughly equal value, and when both **blood signatures** are present and verifiable by badge. **Slavery is banned, but selling your own freedom by contract for comparable value is legal.** Courts decide abuse. Forcibly breaking a contract is a crime — even for Forway or a king. Adventurers are exempt from contract balance during a Forway emergency.
5. **Territory:** Forway owns all land outside the kingdoms and decides what counts as a city or village. Forway cities host an embassy and follow Forway Law. Taxes: cities 20%, villages 15%, kingdoms 10%. Embassies may call adventurers in emergencies and, with permission, kingdom guards.
6. **Production:** no mass production without a granted comparative advantage.
7. **Annual gathering** of kings and city representatives every Paulin at the Forway Palace.
8. **Treason** against the treaty or Forway: up to exile to **Hifuna**.
9. **Essential goods** (added 705 A.G.) are managed and subsidized by Forway.

## Forway Law
Applies in every Forway territory; kings may override it inside their kingdoms.
- **Crime** = proven physical, economic or mental harm to a citizen without consent. **Innocent until proven guilty.**
- **Consent** = written contract, or a verbal declaration in front of several witnesses.
- **Punishment matches the harm**, including sentimental value. Bodily harm can be repaid with equal bodily harm or compensation.
  - **Minor:** restorable within 5 years of contract servitude.
  - **Major:** permanent, irreplaceable, or worth more than 5 years.
  - **Grand:** conspiracy against Forway or the treaty.
- Judged by embassy leaders, village elders, or Forway guard captains. Detention up to **6 weeks** with preliminary proof or flight risk. **Exile to Hifuna** needs four authorities to agree.
- In Forway City itself: if you can pay for your crime, you walk; if not, prison.
- Some legal oddities: the deadly Panita mushroom is legal because Forway uses it for punishment; harming a Nari Worm or its eggs can get you exiled to Hifuna; killing a Forway courier Winga is serious.

### Local flavors
- **Sanbi:** minor crimes → replace what you took; major → thrown into the sea (survive and you're absolved).
- **Tolerideme:** punished by **starvation** (longer for worse crimes).
- **Gandew:** everything is settled by **duel**.
- **Blood:** major criminals become prey for the hunters.
- **Sundeath:** **guilty until proven innocent**; public trials and executions.
- **Flatland:** major criminals thrown into the Skin Peeler geyser.
- **Tastyleaf:** major criminals must go fight beasts; survive and you're absolved.
- **Ganyrami:** nobody reports anything; you sort it out yourself.

## Gandew Duel Rights (~375 A.G.)
- Held in the Low, Medium or High Pit with **at least ten witnesses**. Both duelists state why they fight and what they win or lose. In **marriage duels**, the woman attacks first; win = lifelong bond.
- A witness **checks** each duelist by smelling them and tasting the vein junction at the base of the fingers. Any mind- or body-altering effect must be declared. Fleeing or postponing = loss, punishable by death.
- Duelists start 10 Adi apart. The **attacker** moves and strikes with physical weapons only; the **defender** must stand still, take the hit, and may counter without moving. Roles swap every turn. No healing.
- Last one breathing wins and **must revive the loser**, who must honor the terms or be executed (the body goes to the winner).
- **King-Fest:** only natural-born Darfaries; the king is healed between bouts; to take the throne you must rip out both of the dead king's tusks.

## Embassies
Every kingdom and major city has a **seven-story embassy**: dark stone, accents in the four kingdoms' colors (**red, orange, blue, green**), nari-glass, a black fence and a moat. Embassy grounds are **Forway soil** even inside a kingdom. They're patrolled by **Big Wingas** (guards transformed with nari gel), and ordinary Wingas fly the mail.

- **Floor 1:** adventurer lounge and quest boards · **3–4:** administration · **5:** open roof, mail · **6:** Winga roost.
- **Captains** (always Aquamarine or Sapphire Forway officers) rule the embassy grounds — and the whole city if it's Forway-managed. Captains and generals wear the same armor as soldiers, marked only by a fancy cap.
- **Services:** badges, Winga courier, banking, loans, revival, guild registration, contracts, storage, quests, news.

## Badges
- Official ID and proof of citizenship. **One badge per soul.** Made of greenish **soul wood**; four corner balls (pearl for citizens, or your adventurer rank's gem). Front: name, city, guild. A thermometer-like gauge shows your current **Soul Energy**, visible only to you (your blood and ground nari are sealed inside). Back: the Forway logo. Smells of burnt wood when new.
- **Black wood badges** mark officials and kingdom representatives.
- Blood samples are flown to Forway's **central soul repository**, so a badge can't be duplicated and a mangled corpse can still be identified.
- Cost: **1 amethyst**. Plenty of poor folk never get one — and without one you can't use embassy services.

### Names
Everyone born after 671 A.G. is registered as **NAME PLACE GUILD** — e.g., *Magi Blood Blue-Flower-Red-Bone*. There are no surnames: **guilds are families**. If your guild is "CITY-GUILD" and you live in that city, the city is dropped (*Itagiba Sanbi Royal*). No guild? Just name and place. Sanbians can change their names whenever they like.

## Contracts
- Signed in **blood**, mixed with nari. To verify, press the signer's badge to the signature: the thumb spot lights up if it matches.
- Every contract must list its **nullification clauses on the back**.
- Important contracts are signed at an embassy with an official witness. Kingdoms run their own contract offices too.

## News
- Every evening at **Monnalow**, the local captain shouts the Forway news in the public square. Weekly printouts (*Forway News*, *Forway and You*) come out on Forrday/Freeday — mostly read in Forway and Tolerideme.
- Anyone can pay to post:
  1. **General** (1 pearl): general board + weekly printout. Turned into a marketplace for odd items.
  2. **Local Warning** (1 amethyst): the quest board; announced at Monnalow. All quests are posted here.
  3. **Kingdom Alert** (1 aquamarine): shouted across the kingdom's cities and villages to gather every adventurer.
  4. **Emergency** (1 sapphire): invasion-level. Never used. Requires housing, feeding and arming 100 adventurers within 3 days.

## Signal smoke (Forway-official colors)
**Red** = help · **Cyan** = conquered/complete · **Green** = safe/go ahead · **Yellow** = retreat. Misuse is punished. Bandits fake them to set traps.
