# Daily Life, Food & Festivals

## Taverns and pastimes
- **Taverns are second homes.** Dedicated restaurants are a strange idea; everyone has a favorite tavern, and some Forway taverns are members-only. Adventurers love **tavern-hopping**.
- Bring your own **Picker** (personal utensil) — everyone does, and many decorate theirs. Poor folk eat from **bread bowls** that get handed to beggars afterward.
- **Brothels** are a normal pastime. **Reading** is for the rich. **Theaters** in Forway stage novels. **Tourism** is for adventurers and the wealthy. **Music** is medieval with a modern swing (no electric anything).

## Sports
- **Homeegg** — Forway's big sport. Two teams of five flying on nari gels; four attackers per side fight in the middle and have five chances to carry the ball to a "nest" atop the opponent's pole. Sanbi's team, led by Princess Maiara, keeps winning the finals.
- **Fast Fish** (Waterfall to Sanbi; no flight or gravity magic) · **Fast Bird** (Forway; some gels allowed) · **Fish Catch Fish** (fish while swimming; Sanbi)
- **Gandew Duels** (no soul energy) vs. **Forway Duels** (soul energy and gels encouraged; held in theaters and arenas)
- **Egua Race** and **Dueling Egua Race** (any weapon, enhanced eguas and riders), Forway
- **Soul Energy Art** shows · drinking and food contests (Tolerideme's are famous)

## Games
- **Stealball** — draw a square, put equal amethysts (or better) in the middle, and flick pearls to knock them out (poor kids use pearls and pebbles).
- **Flat Steel** — fancy table Stealball for three players plus a **Table Manager** who uses nari stones to create bounce, slow, fast, sink and dead zones, split balls, or freeze them. Team up against the table and it offers shady deals.
- **Territory Snipping** — darts thrown at once at a board of territories (some colors worth more).
- **Wood Hammering** — drive nails with the narrow side of a hammer (tavern game).
- **Bone Sack** — toss bone bits into sacks hung in awkward spots; take a big gulp every miss.
- **Wood Bowl Spin** — tavern roulette with a white ball.
- **Minari** — a Gwent-like game with carved statues on a 3D board; one statue in each group hides a nari stone (the "true" one) — find and eliminate it. Statues light up with soul energy powers.

- **Asa (spring):** Gandew **King Fest** · Blood's **Great Hunter/Huntress Competition** and **Bone Festival** · Flatland's Hot in the Cold · Deltacross Egua Uniform Contest
- **Paulin (summer):** Treaty gathering · **Adventurer Feast (Paulin 100th)** · Endlessfog Lonllow Cuisine · Deltacross Old Bone and Drink-in-the-Delta · Waterfall **Grand Race** and **Jump Race**
- **Moacir (fall):** Forway **All the Way Festival** · Tolerideme Harvest Festival · Freeish **Blood & Soil** · Flatland **Second Life** · Triangle Fest · Tastyleaf **Picking Festival**
- **Adrian (winter):** **White Roundies Arrival** (Freeish, Sanbi, Endlessfog) · Sanbi **Fish Parade** · Tolerideme **Shake Belly** · Gandew **Weapon Fest** · Blood/Sweetlake **Drunken Sweet Sting Festival** · Flatland **Companion Meetup** · Endlessfog Long & Yellow Sanbi Celebration · Deltacross Egua Uniform Contest
