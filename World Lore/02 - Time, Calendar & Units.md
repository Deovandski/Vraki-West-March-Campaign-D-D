# Time, Calendar & Units

## The Vraki Calendar
Adopted with the Prosperity Treaty and designed by the courtroom scientist **Adrian**. Years are counted from the **Fall of Ganyrami**: **A.G.** (After Ganyrami) and **B.G.** (Before Ganyrami).

- **1 year** = 365 days = 73 weeks = 4 months.
- **Months** (named after the treaty's founders):

| Month | Season | Days | Weeks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Asa** | Spring | 85 | 17 |
| **Paulin** | Summer | 110 | 22 |
| **Moacir** | Fall | 85 | 17 |
| **Adrian** | Winter | 85 | 17 |

- **Week** = 5 days, named after the kingdoms in the order they joined the rebellion:
  1. **Freeday** (Freeish) — first working day
  2. **Tollday** (Tolerideme)
  3. **Sannday** (Sanbi)
  4. **Gannday** (Gandew)
  5. **Forrday** (Forway) — rest day; embassies are closed to the public
- **Age** is simply the number of years you've been alive as of the current year. Birthdays don't matter.
- Dates are written like **Paulin 14th, 1039 A.G.**

### Notable dates
- **Asa 1st** — Forway sells the yearly lunar mapping for hunters and farmers (20 pearls).
- **Paulin** — the annual Prosperity Treaty meeting of all kings at the Forway Palace.
- **Paulin 100th** — the **Adventurer Feast**: promotions and recognitions.

## Time of day ("timehunks")
The day is read on a sundial-like **Time Keeper**. Sunrise is 6 o'clock; sundown is 12 o'clock on the dial. Each **timehunk** is about 3 hours, split into a *low* and *high* half (named after the ratio of nari crystals in the keeper):

- **Day:** Sunnalow, Sunnahigh, Sunnilow, Sunnihigh, Sunnolow, Sunnohigh, Sunnulow, Sunnuhigh
- **Night:** Monnalow, Monnahigh, Monnilow, Monnihigh, Monnolow, Monnohigh, Monnulow, Monnuhigh

Smaller units (quarter, small and tiny timehunks) are measured with a sand-clock **Time Counter**. Embassy captains shout the daily news at **Monnalow** (early evening).

At the table, the campaign uses four simple **shifts** per day instead (see the D&D Campaign rules): **Graveyard, Morning, Afternoon, Night**.

Punctuality is cultural: Sanbians are rarely on time; Toleridemians are on time to the second.

## Sky
The sun behaves like ours. The moon is like ours too, but the night sky has a **green tint**. Focus moonlight onto a small point and living things glow in it.

## Climate (averages)
Temperature drops with altitude. Sea level near the coast:

| Season | Sea level | +2 km | +4 km | +6 km | +8 km |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Asa (Spring) | 20 °C | 15 °C | 10 °C | 0 °C | −10 °C |
| Paulin (Summer) | 30 °C | 25 °C | 20 °C | 10 °C | 0 °C |
| Moacir (Fall) | 15 °C | 10 °C | 5 °C | −5 °C | −15 °C |
| Adrian (Winter) | 10 °C | 5 °C | 0 °C | −10 °C | −20 °C |

Coasts are humid (75–90%); humidity drops about 10% for every 2 km up. The Buhraies Plains sit ~2 km up; Gandew's mountain tops out at ~6 km; the Fuhrair peaks reach ~9 km.

## Units (Vraki UOM)
Adrian standardized every unit of measure from the most accurate ones in use (the hunters of Blood had the best). In practice they are **metric**: kilograms, liters, kilometers, degrees Celsius. Gandew still uses its own units alongside them (duelists stand 10 **Adi** apart).

At the table, **you never need distances**: travel is counted in circles on the travel map (see the D&D Campaign rules).

## Language
Everyone speaks **Vraki**, the common tongue inherited from the ancient Finaies. In practice it's English with its own alphabet ("Vraki font"). Names of minerals are English words, a leftover from a human mineralogist the Buhraies once summoned. **Old Vraki** is like Old English — hard to read.
