# Lands, Ruins & Villages

The wild between the cities is where West March parties earn their balls.

## Adventure sites
- **Bahyum Castle** — the Buhraies' greatest research station, built to be an automated army factory. It never stopped working. A "moody" ruin full of undead (Walking Flesh, Walking Skin, Walking Flatheads); new rooms appear now and then, as if the castle were alive. The village of **Bahyum** next door posts a daily **"Today's Castle Mood"** tracker. When the mood maxes out, undead spill onto the plains and every adventurer is called. Favorite site for adventurers, bandits and researchers.
- **Buhraies Plains** (the plateau north-east of Forway) — a 2 km-high cliff-plateau raised by the Buhraies; erosion doesn't touch it, soul energy does. Covered in pink-red **Wood People** forests. The **East Dip** collapsed long ago (how humans escaped); the **South Dip** road was blown up in 572 A.G.
- **Hifuna** (row 1–2 across the north) — an irradiated wasteland made by Finaies meteors. Everything that lives there mutates into a bigger, nastier "Hash" version, and nothing there is safe to eat. **Walking Heart** stones grow here. Exile destination for the worst criminals.
- **Lukewarm Lake** — Buhraies-dug lake; its giant **Fihi** eat people.
- **Nari Caverns** (under Blood) — home of the protected **Nari Worms**. Noise causes tremors. The **Deep Hole Chamber** is rumored to hold the Queen Nari Worm and chunks of every mineral.
- **Fuhrair** — the largest Fuhraies city, empty and too cold to live in. Doorless snow houses linked by tunnels; Forway science stations hidden around. Valuables and dangers for those who climb.
- **Old Fuhrair ruins** (under the mining town) — old Fuhraies traps still trigger.
- **Ganyrami ruins** — see Cities.
- **Lishin** — the old Lishies city under Forway: an ancient four-story building, an ancient plaza, fighting pits (some flooded, some still used "the Lishin way").
- **Aldemeri** — once the biggest livestock and farm region of the ancients; now ruined plains with bandits, Cruros and small farming villages.
- **Endlessboil** — a forever-boiling lake on Endlessfog's mountain, made by the Buhraies to change the island's climate.
- **Sweetlake Sweet Sting Nest** — an island of giant bee nests and huge flowers. Deadly unless you wear a Sweet Sting costume.
- **Woodland** — dense forest west of Sweetlake full of Tree Trunks (Trunk People) that grow more aggressive the farther south-west you go, as if trained to keep humans out.
- **Newland** (across the eastern sea) — a land of giant mammals ruled by **giant sloths**. Lots to explore and eat.
- **Whiteland** (south pole) — home of the White Roundies. Nobody goes unless they're running from everyone.
- **Sunland** — unexplored far north-west.
- **Desert outposts** — the abandoned watchtowers dotting the desert between Gandew, Flatland and Sundeath.
- **Woods that swallow people** — somewhere in the south, a forest where travelers vanish overnight without a trace (DM's choice).

## Villages
**Around Forway (Freeish region):** Banhia (to-go food stalls on the Deltacross road), Fiera's Hut (an old lady's tradition of caring for every traveler; famous pies), Fork Point (river confluence), Jack Wood (earlier residents blocked roads with trees to rob wagons), Minala and New Freeish (flood every spring; houses on stilts), **Rebula** (walled village deep in the forest; wood carver Nailah and elder Severino), Old Village (very old, very inbred), Cliffbay, Cocona Island, Ganyrami Bay and Winga Beak (lighthouses), Lakeeye and Likenia (south-east of Forway), Bagarisa Hut, Magina Farm (don't ask).
**Gandew region:** Broken Tip (near Flatland; once a bandit nest flattened by adventurers).
**Tolerideme region:** Bahyum village (castle mood tracker), Broken Redtree (north; walled with fost-stone-paralyzed Wood People), and fourteen vassal villages that act as extensions of the kingdom: Tobame, Tocame, Todame, Tofame, Togame, Tohame, Tojame, Tolame, Tomame, Toname, Topame, Torame, Tosame, Totame.
**Sanbi region:** Long Bridge (a very long bridge on the Forway road; elder Tomanius), Fostwater (harbor water glows when salt and fresh water mix), Fastwater, Northpoint, Saltwater, Likenia, Rich Island and Foggy Point (lighthouses), Fishing Tree, Dead Fish, Sanbi East (the Smith father and son are terrible at their job), Green Sea (chief Tupinamba, old and blunt: if he says it's shit, it is), Depoint (chief Manbola, chill old lady), Otherside (on the island across the bay), Sandwich (nobody knows why it's called that).

## Lighthouses
Every coast is dotted with fost-stone lighthouses (pump salt water onto the stone and it glows). Keeping them is classic Pearl work — boring, and every so often dangerous.

## Roads
Main roads link Forway to Sanbi (via Long Bridge), to Deltacross and Freeish, to Flatland and the desert, and up the plateau to Tolerideme. Off-road travel through forests, swamps, desert and the plateau is slower and far more dangerous. The **Blue Wozens** rob travelers on the Forway–Sanbi road; the **Wood People** bandits block roads around Tolerideme with herded trees.
