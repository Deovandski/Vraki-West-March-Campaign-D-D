# Factions

What the public knows. (DMs: the truth behind several of these is in `D&D Campaign/DM Secrets.md`.)

> "They are called Factions because they lack the size to act as they please." — Julina Forway (855–895 A.G.)

## Large
- **Red Winga** — Everyone knows they're dangerous if you get in their way; nobody knows how big they are. Members are tight-lipped and loyal. They're known only by the **choker** low-ranking members wear after signing their lives away in a binding contract — sometimes without understanding it. Forway alleges coercion but has never proven it. Rumored to be tied to Forway's high-class brothels.
- **Pussy Power** — A super-guild formed when many women-led adventurer guilds merged (273 members, self-reported). They protect women and children, but some members play Forway guard and hand out justice they're not authorized to (Treaty §2 Sub 3) — like beating a father to a pulp over a child's tantrum. Forway hasn't declared them wanted; it doesn't promote them either. Easy to spot: insignia worn on the **right arm**. Keep contact minimal and report their actions to a guard.

## Medium
- **Black Magina** — A franchise of banned-drug (Magina) growers. The top collects a cut while new franchises pop up in the countryside every year; Forway issues shutdown quests annually (good Aquamarine work). They never kill — they put everyone to sleep with nari gels. Members hide a Magina petal in their clothes.
- **Wood People (bandits)** — A once-famous guild of Wood People killers turned bandit. They herd Wood People to block roads and trap wagons, let the trees wreak havoc, then loot what's left; they also extort villages for "protection". Tolerideme to Forway.
- **Bagarisa Brothers** — Two brothers whose family vanished on the road became vigilantes, blaming bandits. They want Forway Law replaced with Sundeath's "guilty until proven innocent", display bandit corpses, and attack speakers who denounce them. Sometimes faster than Forway; far more often wrong. Wanted. Secret handshake: index and pinky raised like tusks, touched together.
- **Triangle Lovers** — Three bandit groups of Triangle City merged (with help from the ruling families) and fought Forway so hard that Forway gave the Triangle embassy reduced powers and a special room just for them.
- **Fallen Tree** — Ex-adventurers furious about revival fees who rob Forway treasuries. They stole three nari stone caches worth tens of Sapphires; Forway took their HQ and kills them on sight, but they regroup and spy on quest-takers.

## Small
- **Beer Balls** — A few fat Toleridemian men robbed people for beer money and were caught on their first heist. The name and insignia stuck; now many unrelated bandit groups use it as cover.
- **Blue Wozens** — If you've traveled between Forway and Sanbi, you've probably "donated" to them. Their den is hidden somewhere in the region. Their boss, **Kozaku Forway**, is a near-legend nobody has seen in ages, said to be as strong as a Hifuna beast after fusing with a Walking Heart. Badge: a blue square with a Wozen.
- **Hidden Sorenos** — Less a faction than a secret society. No ordinary citizen seems to have met them, yet Forway keeps their insignia permanently on its TOP WANTED board. Scholars think they're behind the nastiest gossip — some of which turns out to be true.
- **Black Sapphires** — Someone once heard the name and said the group wants a single kingdom to rule the world. Forway spent a fortune investigating and found nothing, which made people suspect it was an excuse to raise taxes. The insignia people draw for them is pure guesswork. Supposedly, Tier V adventurers.

## Big guilds with political weight
**Sanbi Royal**, **Whoop-Ass** and **Dirt-Lover** (Tolerideme's kings' families), **Pharaohs** (Sundeath), **Half-Cruro** (Cruros; accused of lopsided lifelong contracts, defended by Forway as a path to citizenship), **White Bell** (Forway's courtesan trade), **Sandour** (rich Forway family with Sanbi ties), **Hot Skin** and **Sand Sea** (Flatland), **Cliff-Jumper** (adventurer investors), Triangle's three royal guilds, Tastyleaf's four Ground guilds.
