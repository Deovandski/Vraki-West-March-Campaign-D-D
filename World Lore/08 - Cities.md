# Cities

Cities outside the four kingdoms belong to **Forway**: each hosts an embassy, follows Forway Law (with local twists), and pays 20% tax.

---

## Forway — the campaign's default hub
*~100,000 people. Neutral ground, research, academia, money.*
- Built on the ruins of the Lishies city **Lishin**. Officially "not a kingdom"; in practice it runs everything outside the kingdoms. Security tightens around the annual treaty meeting.
- **Inner city** (badge required): the rich, the maze-like streets, and the **Black Castle / Forway Palace** — lower floors are the main embassy, upper floors are for Sapphires and officials only, the underground is research and experimentation. The poor live **outside the walls**, where no badge is needed.
- Big-city manners: nobody talks to strangers; people get called by their profession. **Nari-gel fashion** is everywhere — every color, texture and body mod; the wild ones are for the rich.
- **Law:** pay for your crime and walk, or go to prison.
- **Guards:** crossbows, shiny armor, always in groups, a famous superiority complex (fake weakness, then counter), black honey in the leg pockets for self-revival.
- **Places:** the Palace; *Forway and You*, the weekly journal; **Emelia's Flowery Paradise** (south-west district, next to a bakery); **Broken Knuckle** tavern; **The Wood People Picker** (Yosuke's restaurant outside the gates); the specialized schools; **End Lake**, a.k.a. "the Shitter" (sewage lake to the south; on a bad wind the smell covers the south roads); **Lonida Stadium**.
- **Food:** Wooly filet mignon wrapped in smoked Bagarisa bacon, with fried Cadioca and Domar barley — 1 amethyst, the classic first course in fancy taverns.
- **Festivals:** **All the Way Festival** (Moacir; every city parades a movable monument), Adventurer recognition (Paulin), monthly fashion shows.
- **Guild:** White Bell (Laura's brothels). **Local quests:** embassy checker/verifier.
- **Visitor advice:** bring a friend, watch for pickpockets and smooth talkers in the rough districts, and don't go near End Lake.

## Blood
*~33,000. A hunters' city in the mountains of the Freeish region.*
- Grew around a hunters' fire pit; public **furnaces** burn carcasses and garbage to smelt iron. Retired hunters gather at the fireplaces daily to run the city — there's no ruler, so Blood has little voice at Forway (they once poured animal guts on the embassy). Forway is slowly taking over through adventurer quests.
- Tribal, competitive, welcoming to anyone who doesn't look like authority. Hunting **totems** of bone, stone and fost stone around town; they eat bone meal to keep the soul strong. Some families refuse embassy services and "wood-school" their kids.
- **Law:** major criminals become prey for the hunters. **Burial:** into the furnace.
- **Places:** four rival quarries, the **Nari Extraction Coordination Office** (prosecutes anyone who harms Nari Worms), the **Animal Part Extraction Facility** (Forway keeps prize specimens "freshly dead" with healing spells), the **Nari Caverns** below.
- **Food:** Bagarisa Nose with Cheese (15), Bloody Blood Sausage, bone meal.
- **Festivals:** the **Great Hunter/Huntress Competition** (Asa, two weeks), the **Bone Festival** (vote for totems with empty beer cups; losers burn), the **Drunken Sweet Sting Festival** (Adrian; the top ten hunters bar-hop Blood and Sweetlake in Sweet Sting costumes drinking black mead).
- **Guilds:** Blue-Flower-Red-Bone, the dying Sweetlake Blood-Tribe (its last member is researching shapeshifters to learn why his whole guild vanished).
- **Local quests:** Sweetlake assistance, Woodland exploration, Nari Caverns assistance.

## Sweetlake
*~11,000. The western frontier.* Base for exploring the unknown west. No embassy, so people settle things themselves. Lives off **black honey** and **black mead** from the giant Sweet Sting hive on the lake's island. A snake-oil seller peddles "Sweetlake Bottled Water" ("FII", 30 pearls/L). **Black Gold Trading** is growing fast. Guild: Bloody Honey.

## Deltacross
*~25,000. The great lake crossing between Forway and Freeish.* Twin ferry villages that Forway declared a city; secretly still part of Freeish. Uniformed teams of six Eguas pull ferry barges (15 wagons each; 15 pearls per wagon, 2 per person). Many rich retirees. **Places:** the Crossing Buildings, the Floating Egua and Sunken Egua hotels, the **Calm Water Resort** (broke retired adventurers live there in exchange for guarding it), the open-water **Delta Bar** (summer only; you stand in the water; peeing in it is encouraged). Drinks come in cocona shells you float downstream (10–30). Festivals: Egua Uniform Contest, Drink-in-the-Delta, Old Bone. Major criminals are shipped to Freeish.

## Ganyrami
*~10,000. Squatters in the ruins of the last ancient-species city.* People and bandits live side by side and don't talk about the past. Few badges, no guilds, contracts outside the system (enforce them yourself), and a suspicious boom of goods "of all origins". The **Central Hut** (an ancient Lishies hall): whoever keeps its fire lit is "the leader" Forway must talk to — and they never have a badge. **Buna's Fishery** has never been robbed, and nobody asks why. Inventors of travel **food paste**. Quests here go only to adventurers Forway trusts deeply.

## Flatland
*~35,000. Desert oasis city of Wozefaries and Sorefaries.*
- Founded by a bandit chief who raised the babies launched from Gandew's Goodbye Cliff. Today a few **ex-bandit guilds** rule from behind cute Wozefaries figureheads, and punish anyone who speaks badly of them.
- Open-minded, warm, attentive, handsy, subservient — until you pry into private matters. Then they'll kill you.
- Sex tourism and hot springs: **OutBalls** and **InBalls** spring resorts (run by the **Hot Skin** guild), the **Life 'n' Oasis** resort (bandit-owned), the **Skin Peeler Geyser** (stand near it in winter; major criminals get thrown in), the **Abandoned Docker** (the lake's Fihi learned to eat people), the **Sorefaries Line** zip line, the **Grand Miner** (amethysts for minting), the Darfaries Market.
- The **Sand Sea** guild "captures" Wozefaries and Sorefaries in the desert.
- **Food:** Boiling Ramen cooked in a geyser (7–30).
- **Festivals:** Hot in the Cold (Asa), **Second Life** (Moacir; celebrates everyone who "landed" this year), **Companion Meetup** (Adrian; adventurers meet Sorefaries and pay a fee to take one as a companion).
- **Local quests:** Fuhrair assistance.

## Old Fuhrair
*~8,200. A gambling mining town built in Fuhraies ruins.* Everyone is out for themselves; old Fuhraies traps still turn up. When things get hairy, Forway sends Sapphires; Amethysts rotate in to help the guards. **Falling Rock Tavern**, the **Big Rock** guild (founded by a miner who found a huge sapphire and blew it all on pussy and beer), the "Old Kick in the Ass" drink.

## Sundeath
*~15,000. A desert mountain city ruled by Pharaohs.*
- Freed from ancient races by humans around 270–380 A.G. The **Pharaoh**'s word is absolute; Pharaohs marry only their siblings and choose their successors. Current Pharaoh **Gamal**, his sister-wife **Naunet**, and their twin sons **Anpu and Bata**, who refuse to rule apart.
- **Guilty until proven innocent.** Public trials and executions at the Trial Center; bodies go to the desert. Many travelers avoid the city.
- Shy, very clean, drenched in perfume; gold jewelry for the rich. Mud statues of beasts with fost-stone eyes; **Saynas** are sacred. Outsiders may use only the two great **Pathway** tunnels.
- **Food:** Black-honey-aged Wooly cheese, Purplebread with Sandworm on a stick (~15).
- **Festival:** a weekly Protector Celebration (Forrday) for a different beast.
- **Local quests:** west and desert exploration.

## Waterfall
*~31,000. Mills, elevators and clouds at the edge of the Buhraies Plains.*
- A double waterfall pours off the plateau and makes clouds over the region. Water mills grind Domar flour (from Tolerideme) and fish meal (from Sanbi). "Who controls the mills controls the city" — but the mill families are too lazy to rule, so the embassy does.
- **Elevators** haul people and cargo up and down the cliff (~12 minutes; 20 pearls a person). The **Cliff Walk** is 10,764 stone steps — used as punishment or weight loss.
- Locals favor Sanbians and make Toleridemians wait longer in line.
- **Places:** Above the Clouds Hotel (rich), Longing for Sun Hotel (cheap, windowless, painted suns), Drunk Step Bar, Halfway Inn (couples' discount if you take the stairs together), Finger Cake Factory.
- **Food:** **Finger Cakes** (~7 each; refill pack of 6 for 42–68) in flavors like Gyut Domar, Lonllow Domar, Black Honey Domar, Tastyleaf Cadioca.
- **Festivals:** **Grand Race** (swim from the waterfall's base to Sanbi; any soul energy allowed; record 13.5 hours by a Darfaries woman), **Jump Race** (fastest safe fall from the top).

## Wingarest
*~4,400. The laziest village around.* Grew on the **Great Cluster**, a mountain of giant mud nests the Big Wingas abandoned. Adventurers rest here; nobody bothers with festivals. Pickled Winga eggs (1–2 pearls), the Sleepy Jona guild's famous beds, Old Bert's Bar (dinner only). Propose by teaching a Winga to say "Will you marry me?".

## Tastyleaf
*~3,000. A remote town around a 104 m tree whose leaves make anything delicious.* The tree glows in the rain and keeps Hifuna beasts away; harming it brings Forway's wrath. Four "Ground" guilds fight over the leaves that fall on their side (Picking Festival every fall, using wind spells). Free school if you sign to stay forever. Major criminals are sent to fight beasts. The docks are the only sane way in. **WhiteLeaf Hotel/Tavern** has dancers wearing only white leaves. A statue of the Sapphire hero **Fernan** stands here. Local quests: Newland exploration, Crocus extermination, Cruro reconnaissance, Hifuna checks.

## Triangle
*~36,000. An island cult city.* "The Triangle Way" rules everything, down to synchronized bathroom schedules (public toilets everywhere; enemas at the **Cleansing Fountain**). Three royal guilds with triangle forehead marks run the island (**Ronaldo Lazyeye** — yellow, **Purple-Magina**, **Angry-Jin** — red). Only humans may live here; everyone else wears a triangle amulet. The Triangle Cathedral holds the original rock triangle, said to cure what soul energy can't. Quick Cake (~5) passes through you fast. Triangle Fest (Moacir). The **Triangle Lovers** faction enforces it all.

## Endlessfog
*~17,000. A warm, foggy island where humans and Bruntos live together.* It rains every day. Bruntos taught humans to farm **Lonllows** by planting fish under the branches; four harvest guilds keep them coming all year. Cold to strangers, violent to anyone who harms Bruntos. **House of Bruntos**: bond with one and take it home (adventurers can too, for free). A 20 m wooden Brunto statue. Lonllow Breadcake (~9). Long & Yellow Sanbi Celebration (Adrian), Lonllow Cuisine contest (Paulin). Burials: naked, in the fields. **Endlessboil**, a boiling lake atop the island's mountain, makes the fog.

## Cruro
*~7,000 Cruros in the eastern swamps.* Tribal: the leader is whoever kills the last one (and eats his infants). Rite of passage: kill a Wood Person, build a hut from it, tame a Crocus. Cruros aren't citizens, so they **barter** — corpses, swamp pearls and coral (for lime) for Wooly jerky and food. Ten-ish tribes: #2 peaceful, #3–#6 in constant power struggles, #7 and #8 allied, #9 friendly with the Half-Cruro guild, #1 and #10 too busy fighting Wood People to care. Few outsiders visit.
