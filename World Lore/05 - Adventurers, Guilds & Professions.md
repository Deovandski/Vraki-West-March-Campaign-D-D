# Adventurers, Guilds & Professions

## Adventurers
Officially a **Forway City job**: a decentralized army that keeps order, hunts threats, and keeps tabs on rumors across the kingdoms. It's how poor folk climb to a better life — at the risk of dying a lot. Most adventurers owe money to a guild or lender for their starting gear, and many owe Forway for revivals. Promotions are announced every **Paulin 100th** at the Adventurer Feast.

### Ranks (shown by the gems on your badge)
| Rank | Who they are | Typical work |
|---|---|---|
| **Pearl** (I) | Newcomers; barred from most jobs | Pest control (Sorenos), fauna hunting, gathering, construction and road work in the wild, lighthouse keeping, surveillance around cities, carrying messages |
| **Amethyst** (II) | Working hunters | Wild hunts (Bagarisas), cargo protection, bounties, bandit traps, beast hunts, training Pearls, checking on villages, missing persons |
| **Aquamarine** (III) | Veterans; needs fundamental school or a test | Bandit camps, dangerous beasts, Hifuna control, rescue/revival parties, bodyguarding, archeology escorts |
| **Sapphire** (IV) | The elite; adventurer school paid by Forway | Secret quests, kingdom and guild law compliance, advisors and officials. A party of only Sapphires means something terrible is coming |
| *Black Sapphire* (V) | A rumor. Probably a joke. Probably. | — |

### Revival
- Any embassy can revive an adventurer. When you take a quest you accept a **deadline**; if you're not back, a **search party** goes out to collect your body and finish the quest.
- If revived, you hand over **50% of all quest profit** until the revival debt is paid.
- Die again before you've paid it off and you're **drafted into the Forway Guard**. Flee the debt and you're a wanted criminal. Plenty of people call it slavery.
- If your body isn't found in time for your soul to still be revivable, you're declared **"Presumed Soul Dead"**: Forway keeps your badge, and your belongings go to your top guild (or to charity after a year if you have none).
- Forway guards carry **black honey** in their leg pockets to revive themselves.

### Quest boards
Every quest is posted as a **Local Warning** (1 amethyst + reward) on Floor 1. Retired adventurers and Forway officials rank them.
- **Pearl+:** Soul Energy Assist, Fauna Hunting, Construction Assistance, Lighthouse Assistance, Area Surveillance (cities), Message Spreader, Road Maintenance
- **Amethyst+:** Cargo Protection, Beast Hunt, Bounty Hunting, Bandit Traps, Training, Area Surveillance (wild), Village Checker, Missing Person
- **Aquamarine+:** Bandit Extermination, Personal Protection, Adventurer Hunter/Rescuer, Archeologist Assistance
- **Sapphire:** Kingdom/Guild/Business Law Compliance (secret)
- **Missing Person** quests let you borrow the missing person's badge copy for a week to verify them (touch it to them; it lights if it's their soul). Lose it and you pay **10× the reward** or do forced labor.
- Each region adds local quests (Wood People control, Bahyum control, Crocus control, desert road clearing, Newland exploration…). See Kingdoms and Cities.

## Guilds
- A guild is an officially registered **family** (by blood or not). Since there are no surnames, your guild *is* your family name. Guilds share wealth, property and storage, and settle inheritance in advance.
- Registering costs **1 sapphire**; the embassy keeps copies of members' badges and manages property, assets and storage.
- **Adventurer guilds** share resources and gear. Some guilds *invest* in adventurers — e.g., Biny's **Cliff-Jumper** guild in Forway funds you for 30% of every quest and a minimum of two quests a week, plus insider tips on good quests.
- Famous guilds: **Blue-Flower-Red-Bone** (Blood; Sapphire hero Fernan's family — people think it's an adventurer guild because of his statues), **Two Hearts** (Freeish adventurers), **Tea-Drinkers** (Forway adventurers), **Tough Hammer** (Gandew warriors), **Half-Cruro** (Cruros becoming citizens), **White Bell** (Forway brothels), **The 300kg Club** (Tolerideme; members don't last long).

## Education
- **Fundamental school** (5 years, ~1 aquamarine; free in some kingdoms): reading and basic math, from age ~6. Poor kids apprentice instead until ~18.
- **Specialized schools** (all in Forway; 4 years, 5 sapphires incl. room and two simple meals a day): Cooking, Tailoring, Construction, Accounting, Historian/Teaching, Blacksmithing, Combat, **Adventurer** (6 months; how Forway runs things and threats the public isn't told about), **Soul Energy** (all interfaces; soul expansion so you need fewer nari stones).
- Graduates apprentice ~10 years (from ~16) and become masters around 26.
- Literacy is limited outside Forway and Tolerideme.

- **VI** (richest): King, Queen, Courtroom Scientist, Courtroom Scientist Apprentice, King-Maid (Sanbi's castle and economy manager)

- **III:** Aquamarine Adventurer, Construction Worker, Diener (handles corpses), Cheesemaker, Wooly Herder Manager, Stable Hand Manager, Innkeeper/Barkeeper, Small Farm Owner, Bard, Traveling Merchant, Classy Courtesan
- **II:** Amethyst Adventurer, Boat Repair, Wooly Herder, Common Courtesan, Winga Tamer, Ore Smelter, Wood/Stone Worker, Road Maintainer, Paper/Vellum Crafter, Ink/Wax Crafter, Egua Rider, Hunter (mostly replaced by adventurers)
- **I** (poorest): Pearl Adventurer, Stable Hand, Lighthouse Keeper, Butcher, Tanner, Fruit/Herb Dryer, Farmer, Ore Miner, Quarryman, Lumberjack, Fisherman, Street Cleaner, Waste Manager
