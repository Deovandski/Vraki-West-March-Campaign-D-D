# Races

Scholars sort creatures three ways: **Races** (as smart as humans or smarter), **Beasts** (somewhat intelligent or very dangerous) and **Fauna** (animals). Several creatures sit on the line and start bar fights among scientists.

## Playable peoples

### Humans ("Naked Bruntos")
The most numerous people. The ancient Buhraies created or altered them from **Bruntos** (a chimp-like species) as blood cattle, playthings, research subjects, or soldiers — nobody agrees which. Humans escaped, multiplied, and wiped out the ancient races. ~9-month pregnancy, 1–5 babies.
Almost every human by 1000 A.G. has a little Darfaries or Wozefaries blood, which lets them use the **Darfaries interface** (sigil rings). Rarely, a "pure" human can't use it at all. Some show floppy ears or bigger lower teeth.

### Darfaries
**Giant tusked humanoids** born from Lishies and Buhraies interbreeding. Tall and muscular (both sexes), with a wide range of hair and eye colors (women lighter, men darker). About **1.5× human intelligence** and longer-lived. Most live in **Gandew** under its rigid laws; those who left young tend to be easygoing. They pick fights fast, especially drunk.
- **Darfaries veins** (actually nerves) on arms and back — the biggest of any race. They sense Soul Energy disturbances, humidity, wind and pressure ("if a Darfaries tells you to buy an umbrella, buy one").
- Can soften a fall from about **3 stories**. Can taste/smell leftover soul energy and **neutralize soul energy with their saliva**.
- ~15-month pregnancy. Darfaries parents sometimes have Wozefaries or Sorefaries babies — which Gandew throws off the **Goodbye Cliff**.

### Wozefaries
A **small, slight** version of Darfaries with smaller tusks; adults look youthful. Human-like intelligence, a bit more emotional; friendlier and more independent. Sweet-smelling sweat. **Glide** from about 10 stories. Considered lesser by Darfaries; most grew up in **Flatland** orphanages after being launched from the Goodbye Cliff. ~7-month pregnancy.

### Sorefaries
**Small, child-like flyers** with true controlled flight and a huge reserve of soul energy (near ancient-race levels). Intelligence of a ~5-year-old, highly emotional, sterile, and often senile by their thirties. Sweat smells of roses and lilac. Darfaries call them abominations; adventurers hire them as **scouts and companions**, trained in Flatland.
*At the table:* Sorefaries work best as companions/NPCs; a DM may allow one as a player character for a comedic or challenge game.

### Cruros
**Crocodile-folk** descended from the Crocus, which appeared suddenly in 575 A.G. (rumor: alcohol and a creepy scientist). They **cannot speak** — they grunt — but smart ones read, write and draw to talk. Most are dog-smart and tribal; a rare few are brilliant. They aren't citizens until they join the **Half-Cruro** guild (lifelong cheap-labor contract in exchange). Their hair crackles with soul energy; they can turn **invisible** for a few minutes and feel soul energy as an itchy back. A dozen eggs a year.

### Mixed blood
Human × Darfaries or Wozefaries children look human but carry the parent's soul energy talents. Darfaries + Wozefaries = Sorefaries. Wozefaries + Wozefaries usually = Wozefaries, sometimes Sorefaries.

## Ancient races (NPCs, legends, or deep secrets)
- **Finaies** — the primordial race, tree-born and all-female, from which every non-human race descends. Weak alone, strong together; they can share or drain soul energy from anything. Survivors live **north of Hifuna** and, rumor says, watch the south for entertainment. Speak Old Vraki.
- **Fuhraies** — peaceful snow-dwelling offshoot, single-gendered, live for thousands of years on ambient soul energy, with antifreeze for blood. Considered extinct, but hunted by historians and poachers around **Fuhrair**.
- **Lishies** — meat-eating brutes with no interface, little sense of pain, tribal cannibals. Extinct since Ganyrami fell (maybe some in the far south-west woods).
- **Buhraies** — vampire-like masters of the soul who drank blood and marrow, reshaped the land, and created Wood People, Big Wingas, Fanchakis, Bruntos and humans. They could shape-shift and move souls between bodies. Driven out by humans; maybe hiding far west of Sundeath or in the southern forests.

## Borderline creatures
**Big Wingas**, **White Roundies** and **Shapeshifters** might count as races. See Beasts.
