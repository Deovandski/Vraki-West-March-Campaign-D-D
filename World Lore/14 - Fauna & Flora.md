# Fauna & Flora

Game stats are in `D&D Campaign/Stat Blocks/Fauna.md`.

## Fauna
### Herbivores
- **Cinika** — small cyan butterflies every spring. Swarms paralyze Wozefaries and Sorefaries to lick their sweat, and gather before storms (free weather warning). Gel ingredient.
- **Culuim** — worms that ate a pond's worth of dumped Walking Hearts near Freeish; they flip between heart "modes". Cheap, earthy Freeish street food.
- **Egua** — hippo-headed heavy horse; the pack animal of the kingdoms. Walks on water, shrugs off mud, resizes its legs, and ejects its intestines at predators. Loved, petted, given human burials; only old ones get eaten. Tamed adult ~1 sapphire; tamed foal ~1 aquamarine (carries lighter loads; adventurers walk alongside).
- **Legunia** — desert flamingo-like bird; leaps high, sprints, flees at once. Its fat is a delicacy; its hump tells you how survivable the desert is right now.
- **Lansha** — elephant-hippo of the Cruro swamps; timid alone, territorial in herds. Pushes things down with gravity, oils itself slick, has sour glands. Tastes like lamb; Cruros love it.
- **Monika** — solitary blue-antennaed butterflies drawn to corpses with high soul energy. Follow them to dead adventurers (or the predator nearby). Monika Sight gel.
- **Winga / Pink Winga** — macaw-like birds that eat Gyut berries. Couriers who remember faces and places; sweet meat; their hardened beaks and claws become **Winga dust**. Tamed ~1 amethyst. Killing a Forway courier Winga is a crime. Pink (Tolerideme) Wingas camouflage well.
- **Wooly** — cow-sheep for altitudes of 4–6 km (they suffocate lower). Gandew's milk, wool and cheese; fed Gyut, their milk tastes like caramel. Their death scream carries for kilometers.

### Carnivores
- **Barina** — pond frog with toxic gas from an explosive tail, spike-throwing back and camouflage. Its lungs and glands go into gels.
- **Crocus** — chunky, cute, mean-biting alligators of the Sanbi–Tastyleaf swamps; spike bursts, hardened skin, light-bending invisibility. Ancestors of the Cruros.
- **Fatupari** — a scary-looking but dumb giant tree boa; it launches itself and hovers. Tear its pink webbing to stop its magic. Bitter meat, waterproof hide.
- **Fihi** — platypus-eel fish that absorb and release elemental energy and shock things. The huge ones in Lukewarm Lake and Flatland's lake eat people.
- **Sanbi** — tuna-like fish that give people migraines with radio waves and shed fake lips and tails as decoys. It takes a strong fisher to land two a day — unless you're from the one family the True Sanbi King blessed.
- **Ptaina** — deep fish with a purple glowing head; ultrasonic stuns, compass-scrambling.
- **Sayna** — fennec fox with horned-lizard armor; goofy and friendly ("Pohw-pohhhww!"). Hears for a mile, spots invisible things and sand worms. Sacred in Sundeath; a top export.
- **Wozen** — nocturnal leopard-smart fox predators of the Freeish and Blood forests and the plains near Forway. Silent, dark, leaping, locking jaws. They love Bagarisa — and humans.

### Omnivores
- **Bagarisa** — babirusa-deer; the most-hunted meat (earthy, mushroomy). Fire from the tusks, fireballs, a hot-air crest that makes a flamethrower, self-healing in blood. **Two hearts** (chest and gut) — shoot both. Musk smells like shrimp brine. Classic Amethyst hunt.
- **Fanchaki** — mutant chicken from the first Buhraies soul experiments. Breeds like rats; an egg a day; tender purple-marbled meat. Fast reactions and a decoy body.
- **Soreno** — rat-shrew grain pest and cheap lab animal. Camouflage, voice mimicry, darting, and squeaks that carry far (spies train them). Meat tastes like urine; Pearls eat it anyway because it's free.
- **Yuyta** — hopping gopher-gerbil; whiskers see through walls (gel ingredient); villagers judge the harvest by their plump rears. Screams like a dying human when attacked.

## Flora
- **Basi** — Freeish berry crushed into a thick, earthy, bittersweet pulp. Cheap and filling.
- **Black Sapphire** (black wood) — endangered tree that kills life around it; the best soul energy container.
- **Cadioca** — cassava-like root, poisonous raw; soaked, dried and fried. Farmed in Freeish.
- **Carbey** — the staple grass; sets the base value of a pearl; Egua fodder.
- **Cocona** — invasive Buhraies fruit with unpredictable water and flavor; gel base.
- **Domar Carbey** — purple Tolerideme grain with a cheesy-citrus taste; the heart of their economy.
- **Emerenthy** — glows at night (makeshift torch); stalks eaten like celery.
- **Girdol** — lemony gut tea and gel ingredient.
- **Gosutam** — vanilla fruit with a tough shell; open it wrong and it turns bitter.
- **Greenbread** — huge fruit on tall trees. A falling one can turn you into jelly. Cacao-like seeds; its core binds bone wood.
- **Gyut** — cherry-cranberry berry; picking it sets wages; sweetens livestock.
- **Lakata** — grape that's dangerous to the heart; key gel ingredient.
- **Lehuj** — hangover and toxin cure; a heavy dose kills.
- **Lolish** — ginger root for warm desert-night drinks (Gandew).
- **Lonllow** — caramel-banana fruit, mutation-prone; Endlessfog's crop.
- **Magina** — banned hallucinogen whose visions feel real (Black Magina's product).
- **Nica** — pepper; insect-repellent gels (but you'll smell).
- **Panita** — citrus-scented mushroom, extremely toxic; legal because Forway uses it to punish.
- **Purplebread** — spiky desert fruit; Sundeath staple; rub it in sand to kill the smell.
- **Rakuja** — antifreeze vine that kills Wood People; tangy fruit; its juice gets you drunk.
- **Siguash** — mango-pomegranate fruit; ferments into strong liquor.
- **Soul Tree** — metallic bark, green wood; soul wood. Guarded by Trunk People in the wild.
- **Steen** — Sanbi chewing plant; calming and numbing; tranquilizer arrows.
- **Tastyleaf** — the 104 m tree whose leaves make anything delicious; glows in rain; repels beasts.
- **Tenderthrough** — papaya-like island fruit; cheap gut cleanser.
- **Tibou** — antibacterial plant for soap, shampoo and comfy cloth.
- **Tyr** — nerve agent; worst case, you're locked in your own body.
- **Yabuca** — mutation-prone apple tree (Jonabuca is the prized one). Wood People were made from it.
