# Beasts

Somewhat intelligent or very dangerous creatures. Game stats are in `D&D Campaign/Stat Blocks/Beasts.md`. Art links go to the main #HusbandOutWorld repo.

Adventurers butcher kills in the field to save on hauling. Bring back a head (or the Walking Heart stone and skull) to prove an extermination.

## Aggressive
- **Hash {creature}** — any fauna, beast or person that lived too long in **Hifuna**: bigger, patchy hair, lopsided body parts, half as smart, far more aggressive, usually sterile, stronger versions of its old abilities. Its meat is irradiated — it smells rotten and metallic, tastes like sulfur and singed hair, and turns odd colors. *Use it as a template on any creature.*
- **Sand Worm** ([art](https://github.com/Deovandski/HusbandOutWorld/blob/main/Illustrations/Beasts/Sand%20Worms.png)) — desert ambushers around Gandew, relatives of the Nari Worms. They bury themselves or cloak in plain sight, harden their bodies, spray acid, and grow bigger. The main source of **chitin**; the meat is gamey poor-man's jerky. **Saynas** can sense them, which lets adventurers turn the hunt around.
- **Walking Flesh** ([art](https://github.com/Deovandski/HusbandOutWorld/blob/main/Illustrations/Beasts/Walking%20Flesh.png)) — undead of any species, leftovers of Walking Heart experiments in **Bahyum**. They share a hive mind, regenerate slowly, don't rot, decompose what they touch, and steal soul energy. Away from the castle some rampage, some idle, a few are docile.
- **Walking Skin** ("Walking Bones") — slower, dumber, swarming undead; many were people the castle "collected" during its rampages. Some scientists think the castle breeds them.
- **Walking Flathead** ([art](https://github.com/Deovandski/HusbandOutWorld/blob/main/Illustrations/Beasts/Walking%20Flathead.png)) — the leaders, maybe the physical will, of Bahyum Castle. Lion-smart, see soul energy, nearly impossible to kill, and they lay worms in living flesh. "If you see one, you're fucked." Sell one corpse and you live like a king for a year.
- **Wood People** ([art](https://github.com/Deovandski/HusbandOutWorld/blob/main/Illustrations/Beasts/Wood%20People.png)) — walking Yabuca trees the Buhraies engineered as soldiers, food and lumber. They're everywhere on the Buhraies Plains, sense the world through vibration, bleed pink sap, and lash out with vines that punch holes in flesh. Drawn to **fost stones** — and a fost stone jammed into the crown **paralyzes** them, which is how villages build living walls. Their red wood, rubbery vines, sap and head-sized fruit are valuable.
- **Hifuna People** ([art](https://github.com/Deovandski/HusbandOutWorld/blob/main/Illustrations/Beasts/Hifuna%20Wood%20People.png)) — Wood People mutated by Hifuna. They pulp everything nearby (each other included) and squat on it to absorb it. Sonic booms, razor vines, launched spikes, and branches they rip off and throw to explode. Sapphire-level danger. Their fruit sells on the black market.
- **Teefyro** ([art](https://github.com/Deovandski/HusbandOutWorld/blob/main/Illustrations/Beasts/Teefyro.png)) — a mindless Hifuna chimera shaped and smelling something like a Bagarisa. It fires its teeth by exploding its gums, casts random spells from its mixed flesh, and sometimes detonates entirely, leaving a crater.

## Defensive
- **Koalin** ([art](https://github.com/Deovandski/HusbandOutWorld/blob/main/Illustrations/Beasts/Koalin.png)) — a capybara-like cousin of the Soreno in the eastern grasslands. Males' heads and silky beards beam mental waves: first dread, then urges to kill yourself, then crippling pain. "There's no winning against a Koalin." Almost untamable — but a Koalin raised from a baby is loyal for life. Its brain, eyes and beard make the banned Mind Enabler gel; carrying them gets you questioned.
- **Sweet Sting** ([art](https://github.com/Deovandski/HusbandOutWorld/blob/main/Illustrations/Beasts/Sweet%20Sting.png)) — giant bees west of Blood, centered on **Sweetlake**. Females have no stinger but shoot poison from black-flamed pincers at near-sonic speed ("sure as a spitting Sweet Sting"); small males sting (agony plus an overwhelming sweet taste). They make **black honey**. Wear a Sweet Sting costume to walk among them.
- **Crazed Muckman** ([art](https://github.com/Deovandski/HusbandOutWorld/blob/main/Illustrations/Beasts/Crazed%20Muckman.png)) — shapeshifter offshoots around Blood and Freeish with extra limbs or triple torsos. Folklore says a shapeshifter ate a bandit. Usually peaceful unless you look like food or muck with them.
- **Sucker Lady** ([art](https://github.com/Deovandski/HusbandOutWorld/blob/main/Illustrations/Beasts/Sucker%20Lady.png)) — another shapeshifter offshoot (folklore: it ate a sex worker). It picks a target, stalks them for days, pins them at night and sucks their soul out through their fingers. Drive it off with a fost lamp — or something else finger-shaped.
- **Big Winga** ([art](https://github.com/Deovandski/HusbandOutWorld/blob/main/Illustrations/Beasts/Big%20Winga.png)) — giant intelligent Wingas made by the Buhraies; scientists brawl over whether they're a race. Hunted almost to extinction for their meat and feathers. Families who stole eggs now get rich selling Big Winga gel. A tamed one (~1 aquamarine, if you can find one) is a long-lived companion. They make light, sense life, and raise air shields. The "Big Wingas" at embassies are guards wearing gel.
- **Trunk People** ([art](https://github.com/Deovandski/HusbandOutWorld/blob/main/Illustrations/Beasts/Trunk%20People.png)) — four-armed living tree branches (technically parasites) in the south-west forests. Monkey-smart, deaf, recognize faces, talk in colored light. Their arms burn with adjustable colored flames and blinding flashes. Freeish uses them as street lights and guards; some perform in shows. Enough angry ones can burn a town down.
- **Shapeshifter** ([art](https://github.com/Deovandski/HusbandOutWorld/blob/main/Illustrations/Beasts/Shapeshifter.png)) — living rock-jelly colonies west of Blood, made by an ancient race. They mimic creatures (voices too), merge to share memories, then split. Are they smart or just good mimics? Tavern gossip tells of one that "recreationally" approaches lonely male adventurers. Cut-off jelly blobs sell well.
- **Monica Swarm** — swarms of blue-glowing butterflies that flee bad weather (a blue wall on the horizon means a storm) and strip a corpse in hours.

## Amicable
- **Flinga** — huge pteranodon-like birds that fly high and never land, with unpredictable paths. Stunning screams and air gusts; untamable, unhatchable. Campfire tales tell of a child-sized wooden rider on one. Seeing them in **winter** is an omen.
- **Nari Worm** — huge, friendly, stone-eating worms under Blood whose shed shells make nari stones. **Harming them or their eggs can get you exiled to Hifuna.** An egg restores a lot of soul energy and works like Nari Refresher (and may randomly hatch).
- **Bruntos** — chimp-like creatures on Endlessfog, protected there. Tool users, no fire, great porters over rough ground. Humans are "Naked Bruntos".
- **White Roundies** — intelligent seal-like mammals from Whiteland that visit northern shores every winter. Play fetch, rub their bellies (they purr through neck tentacles), and trade them good food for what they find on the sea floor — gold or a half-eaten fish.
