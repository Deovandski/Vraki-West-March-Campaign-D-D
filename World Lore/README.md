# World Lore — Players' & DMs' Summary

Everything you need to play in the world of **Vraki** (the Four Kingdoms and Forway), condensed from the official #HusbandOutWorld World Lore so you don't have to dig through the main repo.

This is a **summary**. The full, raw lore (with illustrations, spreadsheets, and the light novels) lives in the [#HusbandOutWorld repo](https://github.com/Deovandski/HusbandOutWorld). If the two ever disagree, the main repo wins for lore, and this repo wins for game rules.

## The campaign and the novels

The West March campaign takes place **in the same world** as the light novels, but it does **not** follow their story. Harry, the Sanbi Royals, Red Winga and the rest exist as people of the world, and their stories may show up as rumors, quests, or NPCs, but nothing in a session depends on what happens in the books. The DM decides what year it is and which famous NPCs are around. By default the campaign is set in the **1040s A.G.**, a few years after the novels.
