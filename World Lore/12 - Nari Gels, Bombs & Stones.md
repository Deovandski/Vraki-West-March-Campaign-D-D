# Nari Gels, Bombs & Stones

Everything here comes from the shed shells of the **Nari Worms** living under Blood. Forway protects those worms fiercely.

## Nari stones — soul batteries
- Ground worm shell, heated until it recrystallizes, set into **soul wood** and polished to a point so stones stack into a **nari stone stick**. Full: blue, like liquid trapped inside. Spent: clear and hollow like a diamond (sold in bulk to be melted into a plasticky glass).
- Power spells: **1 stone per Tier I, 2 for Tier II, 4 for Tier III, 8 for Tier IV**, and so on.
- About **1 amethyst** each. Adventurers who move nari stones travel transport-free for Forway.

## Nari bombs
- Mostly **smoke bombs**, tamed to puff colored smoke (10–30 pearls per ~100 g). Some explode violently on contact with water; wrap one in paper to delay it.
- Weak = a small puff · Medium = more smoke and a high sizzle · Intense = an explosion that smokes out enemies, deafens or chokes — and it goes off the moment you infuse it, hurting whoever does.
- **Official signal colors:** Red = help · Cyan = conquered/complete · Green = safe/go ahead · Yellow = retreat. (Deep blue, non-explosive, is used in Sanbi burials.) Bandits fake signals; promotion tests might too.

## Nari gels — wear a new body
Ground nari mixed with water into a jelly, plus animal or plant ingredients. **Eat** it (coarse, cheap, slower, more painful) or **inject** it (fine grade, pricier, less painful). The soul energy is already inside, so there's no upfront cost, but the gel keeps draining you while it works. Your liver eventually flushes the changes (coarse gels linger longer) — but use one often enough and your body may accept the change as permanent. Living too long in a transformed body (say, as a Winga) slowly corrupts your soul.

Every gel comes in four **tiers**: **I Cosmetic · II Localized · III Regional · IV Whole Body**. The difference between smooth skin and having it melt off can be three scoops. **Nari Refresher** resets all gel effects of its tier or lower — every smart adventurer carries one.

The market is cutthroat; new gels sometimes ship with buyers as the testers.

## Walking Heart stones (banned)
Fist-sized black stones that grow in Hifuna's radiation, veined with color. Replace your heart with one and, if your mind merges with the stone, you wake **undead**: no pulse, no eating, drinking, breathing or sleeping, recharging only through emotion (or by stabbing nari stones into your chest). Your skin and eyes take the stone's color, and a flame of that color bursts from your chest in strong emotion. Revival usually stops working. The law treats you as soul dead.

## Other materials
- **Fost stone** — glows with salt water; beasts avoid it; paralyzes Wood People. Lamps (~20 pearls) and lighthouses.
- **Black honey** — from Sweet Stings; extremely caloric and a little addictive; speeds healing and revival.
- **Soul wood** — green, metallic-barked; stops soul energy leaking. Badges, rings, nari casings.
- **Black wood** (Black Sapphire tree) — the best soul energy container; officials' badges. Endangered; kills life around it.
- **Bone wood** — bone, iron, ground nari and greenbread core. Lasts longer than wood; armor and buildings.
- **Wood People wood, vines and sap** — durable red wood, rubbery vines (rope), pink sap (ink, gels).
- **Winga dust** — beaks and claws a Winga hardened to diamond; ground into coatings for tools that "cut through anything" for a few years.
- **Chitin** — from Sand Worms; Gandew armor.
