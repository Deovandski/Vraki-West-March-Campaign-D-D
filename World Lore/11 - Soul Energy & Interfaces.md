# Soul Energy & Interfaces

There are no gods and no "magic" in Vraki — there is **Soul Energy**, and everybody calls it science. Scientists publish papers, peer-review each other brutally, and blow themselves up.

## Soul, body, gene
- Your **soul** is a cloud of virtual particles that holds your consciousness, your **memories** and your **Soul Energy**. Your **genes** are the soul's memories made physical; your **body** is built from those genes. (The author's analogy: soul = database, gene = class, body = object.)
- Strong emotions make long-lasting memories. When parents make a baby, both release a little soul energy and nearby particles form a new soul, so a newborn's temperament looks random.
- **Lifespan** depends on how strong and dense the connections between flesh and soul are. Aging is those connections wearing out. Regrowing a lost limb lowers the density (shortens your life), so **reattach limbs before healing**.
- Souls are toughest in early adulthood (revival is nearly guaranteed) and fragile in infants and the elderly.

## Death and revival
- When the heart and brain stop, the soul stays tied to the body while the genetic material lasts. The dead dream in a loop of their strongest memories or desires.
- As the body rots, the link fades. A soul with no link slowly dissolves: **Soul Death** ("detachment"). Bigger souls with stronger memories last longer.
- Revival is routine: heal the body, restart the heart (a jolt of **storm** energy), feed it something energy-dense like **black honey**. Embassies sell it as a service.

## Soul Energy (SE)
- Recharged by **emotions** and **eating** (eating something alive absorbs some of its soul energy). On average you recover about **one nari stone's worth a day**.
- You can **expand** your soul's capacity with training, like exercise. The rich tend to have small souls — they just buy nari stones.
- Your **badge** shows your current SE (only to you).
- SE comes in **tiers** (I, II, III… X and up). Each tier needs double the energy of the one before it: **1 nari stone for Tier I, 2 for Tier II, 4 for Tier III, 8 for Tier IV…**

## Interfaces
An **interface** turns soul energy into an effect. Many creatures have one built into their bodies (a Bagarisa's fire tusks, a Crocus's invisibility). People use these:

### Darfaries interface (what almost everyone uses)
Made by the Buhraies to control the Darfaries as soldiers. Works for anyone with a drop of Darfaries/Wozefaries blood — nearly every human today.
- A spell is two **logograms**: an **OBJECT** (what it acts on) and a **VERB** (what it does).
- You can draw the logograms on your palm (slow, risky, and forbidden inside city limits) or wear **sigil rings** with them carved in (fast, safe; sold in Soul Energy shops; blank rings are banned except for courtroom scientists and Aquamarine+ adventurers).
- **How to cast:** clench the hand with the logograms → five rings of white flame appear around your forearm → touch your middle and ring fingers to the palm and "eat" the energy (yours, a friend's, or a **nari stone stick**) → each "bite" pushes the spell to the next stable tier → open your hand to release it, or throw your arm for range. It kicks like a gun.
- The five rings hold **2, 8, 18, 32 and 50** tiers of energy. Nobody has officially cast at the fourth or fifth ring.
- **Miscasts:** missing or sloppy logograms make the spell fall back on whatever you're thinking. That can explode in your hand — or turn you into a chicken-like Fanchaki.
- **Verbs:** INCREASE/DECREASE, REPEL/ATTRACT, RESTORE/DISASSEMBLE, CREATE/DESTROY, plus **ENTANGLE** (link sounds so a whole group hears the same thing — used for comms) and **TRANSMUTE** (turn one metal into its neighbor; not worth the cost), both reintroduced by the scientist Gabiro in 987 A.G.
- **Objects:** 26 approved simple objects plus rare approved compound ones. A new compound object is approved only if random passersby can cast it without a single miscast.
- **Common spells:**
  - **Body RESTORE** heals: Tier I bruises · II minor cuts · III deep cuts · IV broken bones · V many fractures and blood loss · VI lost limbs · VII lost head · VIII lost torso · IX near-instant regeneration (if you have the fat or black honey to burn). **Body DISASSEMBLE/DESTROY** causes bleeding, convulsions, explosions.
  - **Elemental CREATE** throws a ball of fire, water, ice, storm, steam, wind, earth, frozen earth, mud, lava, shock, dust or ash. **Gravity** spells pull and push; a Gravity CREATE makes a tiny black hole that evaporates instantly.
  - **World/Object VERB** changes density, attracts or repels, "un-rusts" things, or turns them to dust.
  - **Soul CREATE** — attempting to create a soul. Forbidden-level stuff. (Rumor says a famous Sanbi scientist died trying.)
- **Senses and flight:** Darfaries "veins" (nerves) let Darfaries, Wozefaries and Sorefaries sense soul energy and weather. A white flame runs along them; a black flame works like a jetpack (feet dangling, steering with the arms). Darfaries soften falls, Wozefaries glide, Sorefaries fly.

### Finaies interface (primordial)
Draw a small logogram in the air with one hand while holding two fingers with the other; hold to charge (others can touch you to add energy); release by opening the fingers. Elements only. Fast, animal-like, aggressive.

### Buhraies interface (blood-born)
Logograms **carved into the palm** with a blade. Without them you're editing souls directly — the Buhraies' greatest power and their downfall. Can alter body and soul "DNA"; changes stick even through healing.

### Walking Heart (banned)
Replace your heart with a Walking Heart stone and become **undead** — see Nari Gels, Bombs & Stones.

## Other uses of Soul Energy
Healing, reviving, lighting, refrigeration (ice spells keep meat fresh for trade), drying fish, industrial work in Tolerideme, entertainment (**Soul Energy Art** shows in Forway), sports (Homeegg flight), and the embassy badge system.
