# History

Most history before Ganyrami was word of mouth, the Darfaries burned their own, and the Greed Period lost a lot more. Historians disagree — use that.

## The ancient races (before humans)
- **Before ~100,000 B.G.** — The **Finaies** evolve in the far north, then spread south and reshape the land.
- **~50,000–10,000 B.G.** — Southern Finaies specialize into three new races: the **Fuhraies** (cold peaks), the **Lishies** (meat-eating forest brutes) and the **Buhraies** (blood drinkers near today's Tolerideme). They fight over territory.
- **~6,000–5,000 B.G.** — The Buhraies raise the **Buhraies Plains**, a 2 km-high plateau, to escape the Lishies (fost stones form in the process; Lukewarm Lake is dug out).
- **~5,215 B.G.** — The Finaies sacrifice their army to rain irradiated meteors down, creating **Hifuna**, a barrier between north and south. They later make a pact with the **Flingas** to watch the south.
- **~3,575 B.G.** — Lishies–Buhraies war. **~3,150 B.G.** — the Great Sea War: the Finaies invade by sea and fail. **~2,650 B.G.** — a Buhraies desert attack on the north fails.
- The Buhraies create **Big Wingas**, **Wood People** (from Yabuca trees) and **Fanchakis**; discover **Walking Heart** stones (~2,450 B.G.); terraform **Endlessfog** and summon the **Bruntos**. The Lishies take Endlessfog (~2,100 B.G.).

## Humans arrive
- **~2,100 B.G.** — The Buhraies summon or create **humans ("Naked Bruntos")** on their plateau, teach them Vraki, mix in their blood, and hunt them for sport — frightened blood tastes sweetest.
- **~1,650 B.G.** — Humans weaken the plateau's **East Dip** until it collapses and escape — into the jaws of the Lishies, who farm them as cattle for centuries. Some flee south and found **Endlessfog** and **Triangle**.
- **~1,500–855 B.G.** — The Buhraies build **Bahyum**, an automated army factory that "always had a life of its own".
- **939 B.G.** — Humans steal **Walking Hearts** from the Buhraies (legend: *Strong Walking Heart*). Heroes rise and burn out (*Avenger of Twenty*, *Logical Arrow*).
- **~855 B.G.** — Sabotage at Bahyum plus attacks from Lishies and humans. The Buhraies **unchain the Wood People** (pull the fost stones from their crowns) and they kill everything. The Buhraies cast a spell on the moon that fails, then flee west. Humans learn to wall villages with paralyzed Wood People.
- Humans turn the Lishies against each other: **~805 B.G.** Aldemeri burns; **~525 B.G.** Lishin (today's Forway) falls; **~495 B.G.** the last ancients flee into the desert and, with the Fuhraies, raise the mountain that becomes Gandew. **~438 B.G.** nari stones discovered; **~338 B.G.** nari gels (first used as poisons).
- **0 A.G.** — **Ganyrami falls** to Machado's flank attack. The ancient species are put to the sword. The calendar starts here.

## The kingdoms
- **0–120 A.G. (Indulgence)** — Peace through emptiness. Villages become cities; great adventurers found **Sanbi** and **Tolerideme**. ~60 A.G.: a young fisher makes a pact with the **True Sanbi King** fish. ~97 A.G.: humans fail to take the desert mountain.
- **~250–270 A.G.** — Inside the mountain, the **Darfaries** (born of Lishies and Buhraies) kill every other species — and burn their own history. The first king, **Gandew**, builds the **Goodbye Cliff** to launch Wozefaries and Sorefaries babies away. A bandit chief raises them in the oasis: **Flatland** is born.
- **270–380 A.G.** — Another ancient group raises **Sundeath** and summons humans, who rebel and free the city.
- **~375 A.G.** — Gandew Duel Rights.
- **120–680 A.G. (the Greed Period)** — The founders' heirs fight endless wars; Walking Hearts return to the armies. ~150 Whiteland is found; ~420 Newland; ~470 Sanbi builds **Waterfall** to flood Tolerideme; ~490 Old Fuhrair becomes a mining town; **570** Tolerideme marches on Gandew and a three-kingdom war begins — refugees swell Endlessfog, Triangle, Freeish, Blood and Deltacross; **572** Tolerideme blows up the South Dip road; **575** the **Cruros** appear (a drunk robed figure walked into the Tastyleaf swamp saying he had "a literal awesome fucking idea").
- **640 A.G.** — **Freeish Paulin** builds a secret rebel base where Forway now stands. **648** — betrayed and sold to Gandew; the Gandew king is won over, executes Paulin publicly to save face, and carries on his plan. **650** — the courtroom scientists **Asa the Powerful**, **Adrian from North Tolerideme** and **Bijura Moacir** conspire. The people turn; all three kings fall (Gandew's executed calmly, Tolerideme's starved, Sanbi's spanked in public and dropped in the sea).

## The Prosperity era (680 A.G. – today)
- **671** naming schema · **680** the **Prosperity Treaty**, common currency, Vraki calendar and units; embassies founded; ruins and major cities handed to Forway. Sundeath resists, but gives in and keeps its Pharaohs. **705** essential goods section.
- **~800s** — Forway nearly bans freehand interface casting; by the 900s only fringe scientists still try it.
- **987** — The scientist Gabiro standardizes the Darfaries interface verbs.
- **998** — Freeish's Village Competition ends in a brawl; never held again.
- **1015** — Itagiba crowned King of Sanbi.
- **1039** — A soul experiment at Sanbi Castle explodes; a stranger wakes in a bloodied tower (the events of the light novels). **Flingas are seen flying in winter**, which hasn't happened in a long time.
- **1040s** — *The West March campaign.* What happened in the novels is rumor and gossip; the DM decides the rest.
