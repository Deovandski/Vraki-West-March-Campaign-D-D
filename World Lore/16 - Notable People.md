# Notable People

Famous (and infamous) people of the 1040s, as the public knows them. They're here so DMs can drop them into sessions — **their novel storylines don't drive the campaign**. The DM decides who's alive and where. Birth years are A.G. Portraits are in the main repo under `Illustrations/Characters/`.

## Forway
- **Laura Forway White-Bell** (b. 1009) — rags-to-riches former courtesan who now controls Forway's brothels through proxies and owns a homestead known for "parties". Rumored to be behind Red Winga.
- **Emelia Lakeeye Rich-Flower** (1018) — owner of Emelia's Flowery Paradise; nobody's sure where her money comes from.
- **Yosuke Forway Wood-People-Picker** (959) — grumpy Wozefaries chef of The Wood People Picker, a restaurant for the masses outside the gates. With line cook **Rimir** (1012) and the Flatland twins **Hanako** (Wozefaries) and **Mimi** (Sorefaries), both Goodbye Cliff survivors.
- **Biny Forway Cliff-Jumper** (994) — ex-Black Castle clerk who invests in adventurers for 30% of their quests and leaks the best quests early.
- **Lofingha Forway Amethyst-Captain** (1007) — young, short-tempered captain on a crusade against laziness.
- **Francisco Forway Tea-Drinkers** (857) — Darfaries who lost his family in a Gandew dungeon party, hit bottom, and found a family in the Tea-Drinkers guild. Father of **Takeo** (969), a polite giant (human, though he looks Darfaries) who "questions" suspects with a finger-clipping tool.
- **July Forway Tea-Drinkers** (1013) — the guild's youngest and best close-quarters fighter; inseparable from her Sayna **Mugi**. Don't pry into her past, and never feed her a nari gel.
- **Kaiana Forway Cliff-Jumper** (1014) — calls herself the "Quick-Witted White Caster" (QWWC is sewn on her uniform); everyone else calls her the "Angry White Caster with Double D". Loud, drunk, punctual, reliable; parties tend to disband around her.
- **Maicon Crossroads Black-Dove** (1011) — frequents every illegal establishment and holds blackmail on everyone. Ruffle him and the dirt flies all the way to Newland.
- **Kozaku Forway** (931) — legendary boss of the Blue Wozens; not seen in ages.

## Sanbi
- **King Itagiba Sanbi Royal** (994) — approachable king who stops to drink with anyone having fun. Explored the world in his youth (maybe left a few kids). Do not mess with his daughters.
- **Queen Neferteri Sanbi Royal** (999) — runaway heir of Sundeath's Pharaohs; visionary and ambitious; has loyal followers everywhere.
- **The princesses** — **Yacamim** (1015), the "Manly Princess", an adventurer engaged to Gandew's prince Hiromi after a tavern brawl; her identical twin **Maiara** (1015), even more brazen, star of Sanbi's Homeegg team; **Eori** (1021), the feminine one, gifted with soul energy and nari gels, wants to be a courtroom scientist; **Nina** (1032), tiny bundle of energy.
- **Eiri Sanbi Royal** (1017) — playful, lewd King-Maid and accountant-chef; daughter of the former King-Maid **Coema Half-Cruro** (987), who hunted tax evaders with Cruro help and sailed off to Newland.
- **Sora Sanbi Blue-Glass** (1025) — shy Wozefaries maid with a genius for finances; her parents run a soap shop.
- **Kiko Sanbi Half-Cruro** (1018) — Cruro entrepreneur who founded the Half-Cruro guild. His assistant **Zico** (1023) walks Sanbi every day scribbling in a journal to chat; "you haven't been to Sanbi until you've said hi to Zico".
- **Huneo Sanbi Royal** (Sandour) (1016) — tiny, righteous Wozefaries captain of the guard from a rich Forway family who wants Sanbi to be "proper". Bribery rumors.
- **Lofinghy Sanbi Forway Aquamarine-General** (993) — Forway general and captain of the Sanbi embassy; close to the royals.
- **Kibe Sanbi Royal** (989) — orphan apprentice of the late courtroom scientist **Gabiro**; gets things "technically done"; terrible public speaker.
- **Namia Sundeath** (1015) — quiet woman from Sundeath's servant families; rumored nari-gel abuser who wears animal bodies. Often mistaken for the Queen.
- **Posaqui Sanbi** (1023) — vain, blue-loving Amethyst adventurer who talks his way out of every fight.
- Village chiefs: **Tupinamba** (Green Sea), **Manbola** (Depoint), **Tomanius** (Long Bridge).

## Tolerideme
- **Jimmie Tolerideme Whoop-Ass** (997) — the Booze King: decisive, well connected, always reeking of booze, often found in a pile of big women. His recipe made his family rich. Partners **Kianna** (1004; ambitious, gullible to flattery) and **Isabel** (1011; the one who fixes their messes); kids **Ariana** (1034; hothead), **Amelia** (1036; laid-back, many friends) and **Brandi** (1039).
- **Alford Tolerideme Dirt-Lover** (980) — the Farm King; brilliant farmer, narrow-minded, rumored to have a secret child. Partner **Luny** (997), a Forway-born courtroom scientist; jealous and opinionated.
- **Aurora Tolerideme** (988) — cheerful older maid at Sanbi Castle who still calls Tolerideme home.
- **Morika Tolerideme** (1014) — a quiet, ripped, submissive Toleridemian (rare!) who won't say where he lives now. Separately, people whisper about **"The Stinger"**, a gore-soaked figure in insect-like armor seen wandering the wilds.
- **The Broken-Tree family** — traders **Laramy** (986) and **Honati** (998; illiterate bartender, fierce about her husband), their wanderlust daughter **Dalila** (1027), and cousin **Melada** (1007), a gambler trying to buy back her old life.

## Gandew & Flatland
- **King Nooru High-Gandew Tough-Hammer** (919) — the friendliest king Gandew has ever had; strangely always missing one tusk. **Queen Satomi** (949) — xenophobic, hardworking, gifted blacksmith.
- **Prince Hiromi High-Gandew Tough-Hammer** (994) — a wanderer and drinker with endless stories (he's always the hero), engaged to Princess Yacamim.
- **Tarou Mid-Gandew Strong-Leg** (899) — self-proclaimed "Forfaries" deal-maker; will throw money at any daring plan.
- **Ryo Flatland Long-Blue** (974) — wealthy Wozefaries merchant drowning in debt: "Gotta spend someone else's balls to make yourself some balls!"
- **Katsuki & Katsumi Flatland Long-Blue** (1017) — Wozefaries twins from the Goodbye Cliff.

## Blood & Freeish
- **Fernan Blood Blue-Flower-Red-Bone** (994) — Sapphire bow hero of Tastyleaf with statues in Blood and Tastyleaf; officially died on a Hifuna mission.
- **Akemi** (1004) — his widow, a Wozefaries librarian into the occult; their son **Jonem** (1029) wants to be a historian; their daughter **Magi** (1020) is a narcoleptic Pearl adventurer hunting for her father and paying off the guild's debts.
- **Rebula village** — wood carver **Nailah** (1015), stern elder **Severino** (1007), dreamy son **Vitor** (1025).
- **The Big-Gyut boys** — **Marcus** (997; wants to be boss), **Clodoveu** (984; daydreamer), **Wilson** (1019) — Blue Wozens bandits from Big Gyut village.

## Sundeath
- **Pharaoh Gamal** (974) and his sister-wife **Naunet** (989; "the Neferteri of the West"); their twin sons **Anpu and Bata** (995), who refuse to rule unless they can share the throne.

## Historical
**Freeish Paulin** (rebel, 621–648), **Asa the Powerful**, **Adrian from North Tolerideme**, **Bijura Moacir** (614–696) — the treaty founders; **Julina Forway** (855–895); the **Three Smart Ladies** (Evedyn, Everyn, Evelyn); **Machado** (the Ganyrami Flanker); **Gabiro Sanbi Royal** (937–1039), the great and aloof soul scientist.
