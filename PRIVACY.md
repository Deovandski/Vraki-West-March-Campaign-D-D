# Privacy

## The website

The website at https://deovandski.github.io/Vraki-West-March-Campaign-D-D/ has no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no accounts. Everything on it runs in your browser; nothing you type is sent anywhere. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, so [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) sees your IP address when it sends you a page. One picture on the Maps page comes straight from GitHub too (the original world map in the HusbandOutWorld repository).

**What your browser keeps.** The site saves these in your browser's local storage, so they're there next time. They never leave your device; clearing the site's data removes them, and the save menu can download them as a file.
- `vraki-west-march:v1`: everything you enter in the tools (adventurers, party, trips, campaign tracker, dice log) and whether you're a player or the DM.
- `vraki-west-march:lang`, `vraki-west-march:theme` and `vraki-west-march:units`: your Language, Theme (light or dark) and Distances choices from Settings.

**Offline copies.** So the site (and the app, if you install it) opens without a connection, a service worker saves copies of the site's pages, scripts, styles and fonts, and of the pictures you look at, in your browser's Cache Storage. Nothing from other websites is saved. The copies stay on your device and are never sent anywhere; clearing the site's data removes them.

**Stats for nerds.** The debug page (Settings › DEBUG, `debug/`) shows what your browser tells the page about itself: browser and device, screen, storage use, connection, page timings. It reads this only to show it to you on that page. Nothing is saved and nothing is sent anywhere; the page only asks this website for its build file (`stats.json`) and times three small requests to it.

**Translations.** The website is also offered in Chinese, Japanese, Portuguese, Spanish, Arabic and Korean. Those versions were translated ahead of time by AI (Claude) from the English original and say so in a banner at the top; nothing is sent to a translation service while you read.
