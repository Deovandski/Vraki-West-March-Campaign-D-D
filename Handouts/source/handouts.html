NULLIFICATION CLAUSES

Required by Forway Law on the back of every contract

This contract is void, with no penalty to either party, if:

  1. The cargo turns out to be banned or undeclared (banned nari gels, Walking Heart stones, Koalin parts, stolen goods). The Poster then owes the Contracted Adventurers the full reward and answers to Forway Law.
  2. The Poster lied about the danger of the route or the value of the cargo.
  3. A Kingdom Alert or an Emergency is called along the route. The deadline is suspended until it ends.
  4. The route becomes impassable by the judgment of the nearest embassy (a Bahyum Rampage, a flooded Long Bridge, a Sand Worm season).
  5. The Poster dies or abandons the cargo. Delivering it anyway earns half the reward from the destination embassy.
  6. Every Contracted Adventurer is declared Soul Dead.
  7. Both parties agree, in front of an embassy witness, and both sign below.

Travel by Circles

Days = circles × 1, 2 or 3 (the DM chooses)
Count the circles on the travel map. Fast 1 day per circle · Standard 2 · Slow 3.
Each day: Graveyard · Morning · Afternoon · Night. Each shift, pick one: travel, watch, rest, hunt, scout, repair, prep, socialize.
Food: 1 ration a day (~2–5 p). Winga mail: 1 pearl, about 1 day per 3 circles.

Quests & Deadlines

Deadline = round trip in days + 50%. Miss it and a search party comes (and takes your credit).
Failure: 10% of the reward as a penalty.
Ranks: Pearl (lv 1–4) · Amethyst (5–8) · Aquamarine (9–12) · Sapphire (13+). Promotion needs a quest or the Adventurer Feast.
Get home: start and end every session at an embassy.

Death & Revival

Window: 3 days + 1 per level. Ice the body to double it.
Embassy revival always works: wake at 1 HP + 1 exhaustion.
Debt: 100 p × level; 50% of rewards until paid. Die again while owing → drafted into the Forway Guard.

Soul Points & Tiers

Max SP = proficiency bonus + Con modifier (min. 1). +1 SP per long rest. 1 SP = 1 nari stone.
