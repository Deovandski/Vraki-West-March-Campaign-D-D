# Handouts & Props

Printable, in-world props for the West March: the things a Forway embassy would actually hand you, pin up, or make you sign in blood.

- **Print everything:** [Vraki West March - Handouts.pdf](Vraki%20West%20March%20-%20Handouts.pdf) (16 US Letter pages, light paper tone to save ink). On A4 paper, choose "fit to page" when printing.
- **Playing online:** use the page images in [images](images) as handouts in your virtual tabletop, or drop them in your Discord.
- **Edit them:** the source is a single HTML page in [source](source). Open `handouts.html` in a browser to change the text, then print to PDF, or re-render with `render.js` (instructions inside the file).

Want to know which parts are AI-assisted and which are the series' own art and lore? See [AI Disclosure](../AI-DISCLOSURE.md).

## Tips for using props in a West March
These come from how West Marches tables (and DMs who love props) tend to run things:

- **The board is the menu.** In a West March the players choose where to go. Keep the quest board, the news and the rumors where players can see them between sessions, so they arrive at the table with a plan.
- **Reports are the shared memory.** Have each party fill in an expedition report and post it where every player can read it. Rumors, unfinished business and marked circles on the travel map are what make the next session happen.
- **Paper makes consequences real.** A revival certificate with a growing ledger, a wanted poster with a player's own drawing on it, or a contract they signed and then broke lands harder than "you owe 300 pearls."
- **Give it out in the moment.** Hand the bandit note over when the kid arrives, not before. Slide the contract across the table and wait for them to sign.
- **Make them yours.** Every handout is a template. Edit the HTML, or print the blanks and write on them by hand.

Sources on West Marches play and handouts: Ben Robbins' original [Grand Experiments: West Marches](https://arsludi.lamemage.com/?p=81) series, the [West Marches overview](https://rpgmuseum.fandom.com/wiki/West_Marches) at the RPG Museum, the [westmarches.games guide](https://www.westmarches.games/guide/resources), and D&D Beyond's [guide to making handouts](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/814-new-players-guide-how-to-make-awesome-handouts).
