# Quest Board

Every embassy's Floor 1 has a **quest board**. Quests are contracts: sign with your blood, agree to the **deadline**, and the embassy will send a search party if you don't come back. The captain announces new quests every evening at **Monnalow**.

## Quest rules
- **Rank:** you can take quests at your rank or lower. A party may take a quest one rank higher if at least one member holds that rank.
- **Reward:** paid on return with proof (a head, a heart stone, a signed receipt, the cargo delivered intact). Split as the party likes. Revival debt takes its 50% first.
- **Deadline:** the round trip in days at the DM's pace knob, **plus 50%**. (Example: a destination 5 circles away at 2 days per circle is 10 days each way, 20 days round trip, so the deadline is 30 days.)
- **Failure:** fail a quest or miss the deadline without a good reason and you owe a penalty equal to **10% of the reward**. Lose a borrowed badge on a Missing Person quest and you owe **10× the reward**.
- **Embassy cut:** posting a quest costs the poster 1 amethyst; that's not your problem.

## Typical rewards (per party)
| Rank | Routine | Dangerous | Big |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pearl | 20–60 pearls | 1–2 amethysts | 3–5 amethysts |
| Amethyst | 2–5 amethysts | 6–10 amethysts | 1–2 aquamarines |
| Aquamarine | 1–3 aquamarines | 4–8 aquamarines | 1–2 sapphires |
| Sapphire | 1–2 sapphires | 3–5 sapphires | 10+ sapphires |

Add loot: Bagarisa meat and hides, Wood People vines and wood, chitin, rare nari gel ingredients (Koalin parts are illegal!), bandit stashes (surrender them to Forway for investigation… or don't).

## Quest types by rank
- **Pearl:** Soul Energy Assist, Fauna Hunting, Construction Assistance, Lighthouse Assistance, Area Surveillance (around cities), Message Spreader, Road Maintenance, Soreno pest control.
- **Amethyst:** Cargo Protection (the Alpha wagon escort!), Beast Hunt, Bounty Hunting, Bandit Traps, Training Pearls, Area Surveillance (wild), Village Checker, Missing Person.
- **Aquamarine:** Bandit Extermination, Personal Protection, Adventurer Hunter/Rescuer (search parties), Archeologist Assistance, Bahyum control, Hifuna control, Black Magina shutdown.
- **Sapphire:** Kingdom/Guild/Business Law Compliance (secret), Kingdom Alerts, Bahyum "mood maxed" sieges, deep Hifuna expeditions, anything Forway won't write down.
