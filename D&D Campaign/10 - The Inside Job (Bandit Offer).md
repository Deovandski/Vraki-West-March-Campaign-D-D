# The Inside Job (Bandit Offer)

A random event for any wagon or cargo escort: **Cargo Protection** quests, caravans, the [Alpha One-Shot](Alpha%20One-Shot%20-%20Wagon%20Escort.md). Someone working for the bandits quietly offers the party a cut if they help rob the wagon they're supposed to protect.

It's a **risk/reward gamble**. If everything goes right, the party makes far more than the quest pays. But the bandits might be setting them up to die, guards might show up, and someone still has to walk into the destination embassy and explain why the cargo never arrived.

## When it happens
Use it **once per escort quest**, at most.
- **At departure.** While the party preps in town (loading the wagon, buying supplies, a last drink), roll a d6. On a **1**, the offer comes before they leave.
- **At night camp.** When a Night or Graveyard event roll gives an encounter, the DM can swap it for the offer, especially if the region table rolled bandits.
- **The cargo is valuable** (nari stones, black honey, banned gels): the departure roll succeeds on a **1–2**.
- Or the DM simply decides it's time.

## The go-between (d8)
The bandits rarely come themselves. Someone passes by "on their behalf."

| d8 | Who makes the offer | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A polite **Blue Wozens** "toll collector", hat in hand, on the Forway–Sanbi road | They'd rather rob than kill (+1 on the Deal roll) |
| 2 | A **stable hand** at the departure stables who "hears things" | Knows the party's route and the wagon's contents |
| 3 | A courtesan wearing a **choker** at the tavern | Red Winga hired out to the bandits — or the bandits are using her |
| 4 | A friendly **fellow adventurer** in the embassy lounge | Secretly **Fallen Tree**; hates adventurers who still serve Forway (−1 on the Deal roll) |
| 5 | One of the wagon's own **passengers**, or the merchant's assistant | An inside man. If the party refuses, he signals the bandits anyway (see Refuse) |
| 6 | A very drunk **Beer Balls** copycat | Terrible at secrets: start with +1 Heat (−1 on the Deal roll) |
| 7 | A **Wood People** bandit herding a "lost" tree near camp | Tolerideme roads; the "ambush" will involve angry trees |
| 8 | A kid with a note and a smoke bomb | The party never sees the boss — anyone could be behind it |

**Reading the go-between:** a DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check notices *something* is off if the Deal roll is going to be bad (Sting, Trap or Fake balls). It tells the party "this smells wrong", not what's wrong.

## The pitch
The go-between explains the plan:
- **Where and when:** an ambush spot along the route (usually the next night camp, or a bridge or narrow road ahead).
- **The signal:** a smoke bomb, often in an **official signal color** (green = "safe" is the favorite). Using an official color for a crime is a crime itself (+1 Heat).
- **The act:** "Put up a fight, take a few bruises, run off. We take the wagon. You get your cut."
- **The cut:** roll a d6 — **1–2:** 20% · **3–4:** 30% · **5:** 40% · **6:** 50% of the cargo's value.
  - **Haggling:** each successful DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion) check raises the cut by 10% (maximum 60%). Fail by 5 or more and the bandits take offense (−1 on the Deal roll).
- **Payment:** roll a d6 — **1–4:** paid in balls at the hideout or a drop point · **5–6:** paid in goods from the cargo, which the party has to fence.

### What's in the wagon (d8)
| d8 | Cargo | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Domar Carbey sacks | Bulky; only half its value is portable |
| 2 | Sanbi dried fish and fish oil | Easy to sell anywhere |
| 3 | Booze from the Alcohol District | Everyone wants it; the barrels are stamped (+1 Heat) |
| 4 | Black honey | Valuable; sticky evidence |
| 5 | Chitin or Wood People lumber | Heavy; sells well to artisans |
| 6 | A crate of nari gels | Valuable; some may be fun to "test" |
| 7 | **Banned** nari gels | The merchant was smuggling — he can't report the theft without confessing (−1 Heat) |
| 8 | **Nari stone sticks** or embassy goods | Forway property: +2 Heat, and the crime counts as **Grand** (conspiracy against Forway) |

### Cargo value
| Quest rank | Honest quest reward | Cargo value |
|---|---|---|
| Pearl (hired help) | 20–60 pearls | 2d6 × 50 pearls |
| Amethyst | 2–10 amethysts | 2d6 × 5 amethysts |
| Aquamarine | 1–8 aquamarines | 2d6 × 5 aquamarines |
| Sapphire | 1–5 sapphires | 2d6 × 5 sapphires |

A 30% cut is usually **2–4× the honest reward**. That's the bait.

## The party's choice
1. **Accept.** The DM makes the secret Deal roll. Play out the raid.
2. **Refuse.** The go-between shrugs and leaves. If the go-between was the inside man (d8 result 5), the bandits ambush anyway, knowing the party's watch schedule: they get a surprise round unless the watch beats a DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check.
3. **Refuse and report.** Tell the next embassy or guard patrol. If it leads to arrests, the party earns a bounty of **2d4 amethysts** (scale by rank) and **+1 Forway** reputation. The bandit faction loses 1 reputation with the party and may come for revenge later.
4. **Fake-accept (double-cross).** Play along, then warn the guards (a Winga letter sent ahead costs 1 pearl; sneaking it past the go-between takes a DC 13 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) or Charisma (Deception) check), or ambush the bandits at the meeting spot. Success earns the bandits' bounty, keeps the quest reward, and gives **+1 Forway** — and **−2 with the bandit faction**, who don't forget.

## The Deal roll (DM rolls in secret when the party accepts)
Roll a d10 and apply modifiers: Blue Wozens +1 · party has +2 reputation or better with the bandit faction +2 · Fallen Tree −1 · Beer Balls −1 · bandits offended during haggling −1.

| d10 | What's really going on |
|---|---|
| 1 or less | **Sting.** It's a Forway setup (or a kingdom's): guards are waiting at the ambush spot. If the party *refused* a sting, the embassy quietly notes their loyalty: +1 Forway and it counts toward their next promotion. |
| 2–3 | **Trap.** Witnesses are bad for business. Once the wagon is emptied, the bandits turn on the party, with more people than they showed. Black Magina never kills: they drug the party (Sleep Inducer) and the party wakes up with nothing. |
| 4 | **Fake balls.** The cut is paid in counterfeit balls. A DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) check spots it — before or after the bandits are gone. |
| 5 | **Short-changed.** They pay half the agreed cut and vanish. |
| 6–8 | **Honest thieves.** Everything goes as promised. |
| 9 | **Crashed.** Mid-raid, someone else shows up: a rival gang, Bagarisa Brothers vigilantes, or a passing guard patrol. Three-way chaos. |
| 10 or more | **Sweet deal.** It goes perfectly, and the bandits offer a standing arrangement: +1 reputation with them and more offers on future escorts. |

## The raid
- **Staging the fight:** the party makes a group Charisma (Deception) or Charisma (Performance) check, DC 13, to make the fight look real. Witnesses (the merchant, passengers, wagon drivers) roll Wisdom (Insight) against it. Each witness who sees through it adds Heat.
- **Real fights happen too:** a passenger who fights back, an Egua that panics, a bandit who swings too hard. Run it as normal combat.
- **Witnesses:** the party can let them flee, protect them, or silence them. Killing witnesses lowers Heat but is **murder** (a Major crime). And Monika butterflies find corpses; search parties follow the Monikas.
- Bandit and guard stats: [People](Stat%20Blocks/People.md).

## Heat
Heat measures how likely the party is to get caught. Start at **0** and keep a running total.

| Heat | Cause |
|---|---|
| +1 | Each surviving witness who saw the party help the bandits (or saw them not fight). Maximum +3 |
| +1 | An official signal color was used |
| +1 | Identifiable cargo (stamped barrels, embassy seals) |
| +2 | Forway property (nari stones, embassy goods) |
| +1 | Loud go-between (Beer Balls) |
| +1 | The party spends lavishly in the next town, or shows up with new gear |
| −1 | The staged fight beat every witness's Insight |
| −1 | The party arrives at the next embassy actually wounded or dead (revival receipts are convincing) |
| −1 | The cargo was contraband the merchant can't report |

**Patrols:** after the raid, once per day until the loot is sold or hidden and the party is back in a town, roll a d6. If the result is **lower than the Heat**, a patrol finds them. Who patrols depends on where they are:
- **Forway roads and Forway cities:** Forway guards.
- **Inside a kingdom:** that kingdom's guards (Sanbi, Tolerideme, Gandew, Freeish).
- **The desert:** a lone Gandew guard, or a Sundeath patrol — and Sundeath presumes guilt.
- **Ganyrami:** nobody comes. Halve the Heat while the party is there.

## Getting caught
When guards catch up, the party can:
- **Talk:** the "hero gambit" — claim they were chasing the bandits and are bringing the cargo back. Charisma (Deception) DC **10 + 2 × Heat**. Advantage if they hand over bandits (alive or dead) or return most of the cargo. Forway guards' superiority complex helps: anyone who plays weak and grateful gets advantage once.
- **Bribe:** Charisma (Persuasion) DC 15 plus 10% of the cargo's value per guard. Tolerideme guards never take bribes.
- **Fight or flee:** the party becomes **wanted**. The embassy posts a Bounty quest with their faces on it: −3 Forway reputation, and other West March parties can take the job.

## The debrief
Whatever happened, the party has to report the failed quest at the **destination embassy** (for example, the Sanbi embassy). The contract was signed in blood, so they can't just disappear.
- **Lying** ("we were overwhelmed"): the spokesperson makes a Charisma (Deception) check against DC **10 + 2 × Heat**, with help from the others. Disadvantage if the merchant survived and is already there complaining, or if the party looks suspiciously well-fed.
  - **Success:** only the normal failed-quest penalty (**10% of the reward**). The merchant's guild may post a bounty on the bandits — which the party could even take.
  - **Fail by less than 5:** an investigation. The party is held up to **6 weeks** (Forway Law's detention limit; those characters sit out sessions as in-world downtime) and their badges are flagged. The DM can call for another check later, or new evidence decides it.
  - **Fail by 5 or more, the merchant testifies, or loot is found on them:** convicted.
- **Not showing up:** the quest deadline passes, a search party finds the empty wagon, and the party is declared **wanted**.

### Punishment
Under Forway Law, robbing contracted cargo is harm without consent and a **forced breach of contract**: a **Major** crime when the value is high (anything near a Sapphire's worth), **Minor** otherwise, and **Grand** if Forway property was involved. Typical sentences:
- Repay the cargo's full value (including sentimental value), or work it off in **contract servitude**.
- Lose a rank, or no promotions for a year.
- **−2 Forway** reputation; badges flagged at every embassy.
- **Grand** crimes (Forway property): up to **exile to Hifuna**.

Kingdoms use their own law when the crime happened in their territory:
- **Sanbi:** major criminals are **thrown into the sea**; survive and you're absolved.
- **Tolerideme:** **starvation**, longer for worse crimes.
- **Gandew:** a duel against the merchant's champion.
- **Sundeath:** guilty until proven innocent; public execution for the worst.
- **Freeish:** whatever this village's law is this year.

## If it all goes right
The party walks away with their cut, a lie the embassy believed, and new friends in low places.
- **Fencing goods:** black markets (Ganyrami, Old Fuhrair, Flatland's underground, smugglers) pay **50%** of value with no questions. Legit markets pay **80%**, but each sale adds +1 Heat.
- **Reputation:** +1 with the bandit faction; standing offers on future escorts.
- **The shared world remembers.** Even if no one is convicted, merchants talk. After any Inside Job in the West March, the next Cargo Protection quests require a **bond** of 10% of the reward up front. The robbed merchant (if alive) becomes a recurring NPC with a grudge, and rumors start that "adventurers on the Sanbi road are in on it."

## Quick reference
> **Offer** (departure d6 = 1, or a night encounter swap) → **cut** d6 (20–50%) → **accept / refuse / report / double-cross** → secret **Deal roll** d10 → stage the **raid** → track **Heat** → **patrols** (d6 < Heat) → **debrief** Deception DC 10 + 2 × Heat.
