# Nari Gels & Gear

## Nari gels at the table
A **jar** (~200 g) holds **4 scoops**. Eat **1 scoop for Tier I, 2 for Tier II, 3 for Tier III, all 4 for Tier IV**. So one jar = four Tier I doses, or one Tier IV dose.

- **Eat (coarse grade):** action. Effects start at the end of your next turn. Lasts **1d4 days**.
- **Inject (fine grade, double price):** bonus action with a syringe. Effects start immediately. Lasts **1 day**.
- **Pain:** Tier III and IV gels hurt as your body reshapes. Coarse grade: take 2d6 (Tier III) or 4d6 (Tier IV) psychic damage and you're poisoned until the end of your next turn. Fine grade: no damage.
- **Drain:** Tier I–II gels cost nothing extra. While a **Tier III** gel is active you don't regain SP on long rests. While a **Tier IV** gel is active you lose 1 SP each dawn (at 0 SP, gain a level of exhaustion instead).
- **Stacking:** a few different gels can stack (DM's call); the same gel doesn't stack with itself — use the higher tier.
- **Ending early:** a **Nari Refresher** of equal or higher tier ends all gel effects on you.
- **Getting stuck:** use the same Tier III+ gel for more than 3 days in a row and make a DC 13 Constitution save each extra day; on a failure the change becomes **permanent** until a Nari Refresher of that tier and a long rest. Living permanently transformed for months corrupts the soul (DM's call: you may never come back).

## Gear and materials
Use PHB equipment and prices (1 gp = 10 pearls), with Vraki materials:
- **Bone wood** (bone, iron, ground nari, greenbread core): as wood or metal; lasts longer. Freeish guards wear bone-wood armor (as hide/scale).
- **Chitin** (Sand Worm): Gandew armor. Treat chitin plate as half plate with no disadvantage on Stealth; price ×1.5.
- **Wood People vines**: as hempen rope but elastic (50 ft for 5 pearls in Tolerideme, 15 elsewhere).
- **Winga-dust coating**: a weapon coated with Winga dust ignores resistance to slashing/piercing from nonmagical attacks for a year. 1 aquamarine.
- **Badge replacement**: 1 amethyst (plus questions).
- **Sigil rings**: see Soul Energy rules.
