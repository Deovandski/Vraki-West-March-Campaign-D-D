# Soul Energy, Interfaces & Sigil Rings

Magic in Vraki is **soul energy** pushed through an **interface**. These rules sit on top of normal 5e spellcasting: class casters keep their spell slots; everyone else gets access to magic through **sigil rings** and **nari stones**.

## Tiers = spell levels
A spell's **level** is its **Tier** (a 3rd-level spell is a Tier III spell). Cantrips are Tier 0. Energy doubles each tier:

| Tier | I | II | III | IV | V | VI | VII | VIII | IX |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Nari stones / SP needed** | 1 | 2 | 4 | 8 | 16 | 32 | 64 | 128 | 256 |

## Soul Points (SP)
- **Maximum SP** = proficiency bonus + Constitution modifier (minimum 1). Your badge shows your current SP.
- **1 SP = 1 nari stone's worth of energy.**
- **Recovering SP:**
  - Finish a long rest: regain **1 SP** (about a nari stone's worth a day, as in the lore).
  - **Strong emotion:** when the DM awards Inspiration, or something truly moving, terrifying, triumphant or lustful happens to you, regain 1 SP (DM's call; max once per day).
  - **Eat something alive** (or freshly killed) that has a soul: regain 1 SP (once per day).
  - A **Nari Worm egg** restores up to 4 SP.
- **Expanding your soul:** once per level, a character who spends a downtime week training at a Soul Energy school (or with a mentor) increases their maximum SP by 1 (maximum +3 total).
- **Soul drain:** some monsters drain SP. If a drain would take you below 0 SP, you gain a level of exhaustion instead.

## Casting with spell slots (class casters)
Unchanged. Your class magic is your soul energy flowing through your interface; even a "pure" human or a Cruro caster has learned to shape their soul directly. Describe your spells as interfaces — rings of white flame around your forearm, the "eating" gesture, the kick when you release.

**Fueling slots with stones or SP:** as part of casting a spell you know or have prepared, a caster may spend **nari stones and/or SP equal to the Tier** (table above) instead of expending a spell slot. You can only do this for a Tier you have spell slots for, and you can do it **as often as you have the stones** — stones are expensive, and that's the limit.

## Sigil rings (everyone with a Darfaries interface)
Sigil rings carry a spell's logograms (OBJECT + VERB). Anyone with the **Darfaries Interface** trait can cast a ring's spell:
- **Action** (or the spell's casting time, if longer). Clench, "eat" the energy, release.
- **Cost:** spend nari stones and/or SP for the ring's Tier (table above). Cantrip rings cost 1 SP or 1 stone per cast.
- **Spell attack bonus** = your proficiency bonus + your highest mental modifier (Int, Wis or Cha). **Save DC** = 8 + the same.
- You can wear up to **four** rings (two per hand). Swapping a ring takes an action.
- A ring is set to one Tier when carved; a higher-Tier ring of the same spell costs more.
- **Banned:** blank rings (except for courtroom scientists and Aquamarine+ adventurers), and destructive Body rings outside guard/army use.

## Drawn logograms (risky)
Without a ring, you can draw the logograms on your palm (1 minute, needs ink or a blade). Make an Intelligence (Arcana) check, **DC 10 + 2 × Tier**. On a failure, the spell defaults to whatever's on your mind: the DM picks a weird result, or it **explodes** for 1d6 fire damage per Tier to you. Forbidden inside city and kingdom limits.

## Other interfaces
- **Darfaries veins** sense soul energy (see Making an Adventurer).
- **Buhraies interface** (logograms carved into the palm) can alter bodies and souls permanently. Only for NPCs, villains, or an epic campaign reward.
- **Finaies interface** — air-drawn elemental spells; nobody living in the south knows it.

## Revival magic
To revive someone in the field you need **Body RESTORE** to fix the body (enough Tier to heal the damage), **Storm CREATE** to restart the heart, and something energy-dense like **black honey** to feed the healing. Mechanically: *revivify* (Tier III) within its normal limit, or the embassy's service. See [Death, Revival & Debt](05%20-%20Death,%20Revival%20&%20Debt.md).
