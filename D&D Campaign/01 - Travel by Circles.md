# Travel by Circles

Forget kilometers, Egua speeds and load math. The **travel map** (see [Maps](../Maps)) has **circles drawn along the routes connecting places**. Travel is counted in circles.

## 1. Count circles
Follow the circles from where the party is to where it wants to go and count them. That count is the length of the trip. (How many circles sit between any two places is whatever the map shows.)

Going somewhere with no circles drawn — off the routes, into unexplored land? The DM decides how many circles the trip is worth, or draws new ones on the shared map.

## 2. Set the pace knob
**Each circle represents 1, 2 or 3 days of travel. The DM chooses**, trip by trip, to make the journey shorter or longer:

| Pace | Days per circle | Good fit when… |
|---|---|---|
| **Fast** | **1** | Good roads, riding Eguas, traveling light, boats on open sea, or the DM wants to get to the action |
| **Standard** | **2** | Normal travel: walking or a wagon on roads and trails, mixed terrain |
| **Slow** | **3** | Off-road forest, swamp, desert, mountains, the Buhraies Plains, heavy wagons or caravans, bad weather, Hifuna, or the DM wants the journey to *be* the adventure |

The knob is a **storytelling tool**, not physics. The DM can set it once for the whole trip or change it per leg when the terrain changes (Fast along a road, Slow once the party heads into the swamp).

> **Days = circles × the DM's pace (1, 2 or 3).**

## 3. Play out the days (or skip them)
Each day has four **shifts**, kept from the Alpha campaign:

| Shift | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Graveyard** | 12am–6am | Decide who keeps watch (if anyone). The DM rolls for events. |
| **Morning** | 6am–12pm | Players choose actions. The DM may roll for events. |
| **Afternoon** | 12pm–6pm | Players choose actions. The DM may roll for events. |
| **Night** | 6pm–12am | Players choose actions. The DM rolls for events. |

Each shift, every character picks one thing: **travel**, **keep watch**, **rest**, **hunt/forage**, **scout** (with a companion like a Sayna, Winga or Sorefaries), **repair**, **prep** (nari gels, sigil rings, traps), or **socialize** (in town or with other caravans: barter, flirt, gamble, gossip, steal). Travel is assumed to happen during the day; the circle count already covers it.

Event rolls are in [Events & Encounters](07%20-%20Events%20&%20Encounters.md). To keep long trips quick, the DM can roll **once per circle** instead of every shift — another knob.

## Supplies
- **Food:** 1 ration per person per day (~2–5 pearls in town). Hunting/foraging for a shift: DC 12 Wisdom (Survival) feeds 1d4 people for a day (DC 15 in Slow-pace country).
- **Eguas:** no speed or load math. An Egua lets the party carry loot and gear (a pack Egua carries what a wagon would) and is a good reason for **Fast** pace on roads. It eats by grazing during Morning and Night; in deserts or Hifuna, bring Carbey (about 1 pearl a day). A wagon on bad terrain is a good reason for Slow.
- **Water:** only matters in the desert and on the plateau's dry stretches (see Dangerous Places).

## Getting lost
Off the drawn routes, or at Slow pace in the wild, the navigator makes a DC 12 Wisdom (Survival) check per circle (DC 15 in fog, desert storms or Hifuna). On a failure, that circle takes **one extra day**. Maps (1 amethyst; 1 aquamarine for detailed ones) or a local guide grant advantage.

## Pushing it
The party can push one step faster than the DM's knob (Slow → Standard, Standard → Fast) for a circle. Everyone makes a DC 12 Constitution save at the end of that circle or gains a level of exhaustion. Eguas pushed too hard get cranky (and kick).

## Ferries, elevators, boats and mail
- The **Deltacross ferry** and the **Waterfall elevators** don't add time; they cost balls (see World Lore: Money).
- **Boats** along the coast or between islands are usually a good fit for **Fast**. Passage costs about 5 pearls per person per circle on a merchant boat.
- **Winga mail** flies much faster than people: about **one day for every three circles**.

## Quick reference
> **Count the circles. Each circle is 1, 2 or 3 days — the DM chooses.** Roll events per shift (or per circle). Feed people and eguas. Get home before the quest deadline.
