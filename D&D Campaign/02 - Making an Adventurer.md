# Making an Adventurer

Use the normal D&D 5e character creation rules (2014 or 2024), with the changes below. Characters start at **level 1** as **Pearl** adventurers, unless the DM says otherwise. Pre-generated level 3 adventurers are in [Adventurers](Adventurers).

## 1. Pick your people
Vraki has no elves, dwarves, halflings or gnomes. Use these instead. Unless noted, use the standard **human** chassis for size, speed and languages (Vraki is the common tongue), then apply the traits below.

### Human (mixed blood) — the default
Almost every human has a little Darfaries or Wozefaries blood.
- Use the 5e **Human** (any version your table uses).
- **Darfaries Interface.** You can use sigil rings and drawn logograms (see Soul Energy rules).
- **Heritage hint** (optional, pick one): *Darfaries blood* — proficiency in Athletics; or *Wozefaries blood* — proficiency in Acrobatics.

### "Pure" human (rare)
No Darfaries or Wozefaries blood at all — maybe from a remote bloodline, or somewhere stranger.
- Use the 5e **Human**.
- **No Interface.** You can't use sigil rings or logograms. (Class spellcasting still works — see Soul Energy rules for why.)
- **Deep Soul.** You regain **1 extra Soul Point** when you finish a long rest.

### Darfaries
Giant tusked people; most come from Gandew.
- **Size** Medium (2–2.5 m tall). **Speed** 30 ft.
- **Powerful Build.** You count as one size larger for carrying, pushing, dragging and lifting.
- **Tusks.** Natural weapon: 1d6 + Strength piercing.
- **Darfaries Veins.** You sense soul energy: you know when a spell is cast or a nari gel is used within 30 ft, and you can tell the weather coming in the next day (no check).
- **Soften the Fall.** Reaction when falling: take no damage from the first 30 ft of a fall.
- **Neutralizing Saliva.** Once per long rest, as an action, lick a creature or object you touch to end one ongoing nari gel or spell effect of Tier/level 2 or lower on it.
- **Darfaries Interface.**
- **Short Fuse.** When you're drunk (DM's call), you have disadvantage on saves to resist being goaded into a fight.

### Wozefaries
Small, slight cousins of the Darfaries; most grew up in Flatland after the Goodbye Cliff.
- **Size** Small or Medium (your choice). **Speed** 30 ft.
- **Glide.** When you fall, you can descend at 60 ft per round and move up to 2 ft horizontally for every 1 ft you descend; you take no falling damage while gliding. You can't gain altitude.
- **Darfaries Veins** (as above, 30 ft).
- **Neutralizing Saliva** (as above).
- **Friendly Face.** Proficiency in Persuasion or Insight.
- **Darfaries Interface.**

### Cruro
Crocodile-folk from the Cruro swamps who joined (or escaped to) society. You're only a citizen if you belong to the Half-Cruro guild.
- **Size** Medium. **Speed** 30 ft, **swim** 30 ft.
- **Mute.** You can't speak. You understand Vraki and communicate by writing, drawing, gestures and grunts that other Cruros and Crocuses understand.
- **Scaly Hide.** Without armor, your AC is 13 + your Dexterity modifier.
- **Bite.** Natural weapon: 1d6 + Strength piercing.
- **Hold Breath.** 15 minutes.
- **Fade.** Once per long rest, as an action, you turn invisible for 1 minute or until you attack or cast a spell.
- **Itchy Back.** You feel soul energy being used within 30 ft as an itch (you know it's happening, not where).
- **Cruro Interface.** You can't use sigil rings (no Darfaries blood), but your Fade and Itchy Back use soul energy.

### Sorefaries (DM's permission)
Tiny, joyful, child-minded flyers. Best as companions, but a DM can allow one for a chaotic game.
- **Size** Small. **Speed** 25 ft, **fly** 30 ft (only in no armor or light armor).
- **Huge Soul.** Your maximum Soul Points increase by 2.
- **Childlike Mind.** Disadvantage on Intelligence (Investigation) and Wisdom (Insight) checks. Your emotions run loud: the DM may call for a Wisdom save to stay calm when things get scary or exciting.
- **Darfaries Veins** (as above). **Darfaries Interface.**
- **Sterile & Short-lived.** Flavor only: Sorefaries grow senile in their thirties.

### Companions instead of pets
Saynas, Wingas, Bruntos, Sorefaries and the occasional Big Winga are classic adventurer companions. Use the stat blocks as beast companions/familiars where your class allows, or as hirelings.

## 2. Pick a class
Every class works. Magic is reskinned as **soul energy science**:
- **Wizards, sorcerers, bards, artificers:** soul energy scientists and performers casting through interfaces.
- **Clerics and paladins:** there are no gods. Make them **soul medics**, ancestor-revering duelists, oath-bound contract enforcers, or devotees of the sea, the Smart Ladies, the Tastyleaf tree or the Triangle. Their power is still soul energy.
- **Warlocks:** your patron is a **contract** — signed in blood with a Black Sapphire, Red Winga's Madam, a Finaies watching from the north, Bahyum Castle itself, or something worse.
- **Druids and rangers:** hunters of Blood, Freeish Soil-Group farmers, Endlessfog Brunto-friends. Wild Shape is "soul shaping" (or you're just really good with nari gels).
- **Monks:** Gandew duelists, Tea-Drinkers brawlers.
- **Rogues:** guild agents, Red Winga runaways, Ganyrami squatters.

Spell names are descriptive, not shouted. A fireball is an **Elemental Fire CREATE** at Tier III.

## 3. Pick a background
Any 5e background works with a Vraki twist. Ideas: **Goodbye Cliff Survivor** (Wozefaries/Sorefaries raised in Flatland), **Guild Debtor**, **Blood Hunter**, **Sanbi Fisher**, **Toleridemian Brewer**, **Gandew Duelist**, **Freeish Villager** (Soil or Blood group), **Embassy Clerk**, **Runaway Courtesan**, **Contract Servant** (you sold your freedom and bought it back), **Ex-Bandit** (Beer Balls, Wood People…), **Half-Cruro Laborer**, **Soul Energy School Dropout**, **Lighthouse Keeper**.

## 4. Your badge, name and guild
- Everyone starts with an embassy **badge** (Pearl corners). It shows your Soul Points to you alone.
- Your name is **NAME PLACE GUILD** (e.g., *Tosa Blood Red-Fang*). No surname — your guild is your family. No guild? Just name and place.
- Tolerideme names have 4 or 6 letters; Gandew names sound Chinese/Japanese and your place must be High-, Mid- or Low-Gandew; Sanbi names are unique and changeable; Freeish and Sanbi names often sound Brazilian.

## 5. Money
Prices in the PHB convert easily:

| Ball | 5e equivalent |
|---|---|
| 1 **Pearl** | 1 sp |
| 10 Pearls | 1 gp |
| 1 **Amethyst** (50 pearls) | 5 gp |
| 1 **Aquamarine** (500 pearls) | 50 gp |
| 1 **Sapphire** (5,000 pearls) | 500 gp |

Anything cheaper than a pearl (a bread bowl, a spoon of Basi) is "a few crumbs" — round it off.

**Starting money:** take your class's starting equipment plus **2d4 × 10 pearls**.

## 6. Starting debt (recommended)
Most adventurers owe someone for their gear. Roll 1d6:
1–2: no debt (lucky you) · 3–4: **1 amethyst** owed to your guild · 5: **2 amethysts** owed to a private lender (who's not nice) · 6: **1 aquamarine** owed to an investor guild like the Cliff-Jumpers (they take 30% of your quest pay until it's paid).

## 7. Ranks and levels
| Rank | Levels | Quest access | Badge corners |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pearl** | 1–4 | Pearl quests | Pearl |
| **Amethyst** | 5–8 | + Amethyst quests | Amethyst |
| **Aquamarine** | 9–12 | + Aquamarine quests | Aquamarine |
| **Sapphire** | 13+ | + Sapphire and secret quests | Sapphire |

Reaching the level isn't enough. You also need a **promotion**: complete a promotion quest set by the embassy, or get recognized at the **Adventurer Feast** (Paulin 100th). Aquamarine needs fundamental schooling or passing a test (DC 13 Intelligence check, or a scene). Sapphire needs Adventurer School — Forway pays, but it takes half a year of in-world time (and Forway now considers you *theirs*).

## 8. Soul Points
Every character has **Soul Points (SP)** = **proficiency bonus + Constitution modifier** (minimum 1). See [Soul Energy, Interfaces & Sigil Rings](03%20-%20Soul%20Energy,%20Interfaces%20&%20Sigil%20Rings.md).
