# DM Secrets

**Players: stop reading.** These are the hidden truths of the world, taken from the main #HusbandOutWorld lore and story notes. Use, change or ignore them; the West March doesn't depend on the novels.

## Black Sapphires (they're real)
- Founded by **three Sapphire adventurers**, now including a handful of Forway city officials. Goal: unify Forway into a single nation and topple every king — "finishing what the courtroom scientists started".
- Their plans are slow, so they research **eternal life**. In **Fuhrair** they keep **Fuhraies prisoners** in a hidden torture lab, studying how they live for thousands of years.
- The Sapphire hero **Fernan Blood** found that prison. His Black Sapphire colleagues killed him and hid the body; officially he died in Hifuna. A look-alike carried his armor and bow to Tastyleaf to bury the trail. His daughter **Magi** is still searching. A Blue Wozens bandit, **Wilson**, once tried to steal Fernan's bow from the group, got punched senseless, and later built a replica — a clue.
- They recruit only trustworthy Sapphires and leave no paper trail. Forway's loud "investigation" of them was partly cover.

## Red Winga
- The Madam is **Laura Forway White-Bell**. Proxies like **Emelia Lakeeye Rich-Flower** (Emelia's Flowery Paradise) run front businesses. Ranks: Madam → Proxies (3+) → **Agents** (10+, infiltrate rich households and courts) → **Entertainers** (80+).
- They grab Wozefaries babies in the desert before Flatland does, and lure young people in with promises. Raised in proxy businesses, they sign contracts (often with forged Forway overlays). Disobedient ones are forced into "entertainment" and must wear the **choker** — it's a mark of distrust, not membership.
- Creed: "We are here to please you." Always watch your surroundings. Agents who rise become proxies.
- Laura's rule: sell information, don't make enemies of black-badge officials.

## Hidden Sorenos
Four of Forway's richest aristocrats and 15+ trusted agents who quietly ruin rivals without paper or blood trails. Slow, precise. They use trained Sorenos to relay messages and hide a Soreno badge in their underwear.

## Blue Wozens
- Boss **Kozaku Forway** (b. 931), mixed Human–Darfaries, bitter about Darfaries smugness and a rich family that screwed him with a contract. He fused with a red **Walking Heart**: one-punch head-ripping strength, rage he barely controls, and a soul that can burn out if nobody stops him. He's forgotten why he founded the gang.
- The **den** is in the forest west of Forway (near Fiera's Hut / Fork Point on the Metadata map).
- Squads: 1st (5), 2nd (**Marcus**, **Wilson**, **Clodoveu Big-Gyut**), 3rd (3). Red Winga sometimes hires them.

## Bahyum
The castle is the Buhraies' automated army factory — "it always had a life of its own". The Walking Flatheads may be what took control from the Buhraies. Its **Walking Skin** includes many humans it "sequestered"; the castle may be breeding new undead through Walking Hearts.

## The north is watching
The **Finaies** still live north of Hifuna. They have a pact with the **Flingas** and watch the south like a reality show. The "wooden child rider" in campfire tales is a Finaies scout. Flingas flying in winter means the Finaies are paying closer attention.

## Hidden ancients
- A Buhraies family hid on **Endlessfog**, running a small Lonllow farm with secret "exotic" recipes (they forced Bruntos into labor and sex rituals and cooked with what they extracted). A ritual went wrong and destroyed the family; the survivor, **Perderhora** (b. 769), burned the farm. A Forway farmer captured him, Gabiro experimented on him (he's now mute, burrows with his hands, and can share memories by clawing into a head), and he escaped a freak show when Pussy Power raided it near Deltacross. He's out there.
- Remnant Buhraies may live far west of Sundeath or in the southern woods; a few Lishies may survive in the far south-west woodland.

## Flatland's ruling guilds
Ex-bandit families still run underground operations from the **Life 'n' Oasis** resort, put cute Wozefaries out front, and kill anyone who pries. The **Sand Sea** guild kidnaps Wozefaries and Sorefaries in the desert; some end up with Red Winga.

## Royal secrets (useful for intrigue)
- King **Itagiba** fathered bastards as "Juno Triangle Trader" — at least **Kaiana Cliff-Jumper**. He'd pay to know the others.
- Queen **Neferteri** runs a network of agents (Posaqui, Namia, Sinoi, Morika…) to protect her image and her children; she invented the "Sanbi Eye" post to cover a scandal.
- Farm King **Alford** has a secret child with **Aurora**, now a Sanbi maid; Itagiba and Eiri arranged it so Luny never finds out.
- Gandew's King **Nooru** chases Wozefaries in secret; Queen Satomi breaks one of his tusks every time he's healed. The twins **Katsuki and Katsumi** are actually their children, launched from the Goodbye Cliff.
- **Morika** is **The Stinger**.
- **Takeo** isn't a Darfaries (no veins) and eats Hifuna fruit for power.
- **July** of the Tea-Drinkers was born male and keeps her body with gels; she killed a man who forced Nari Refresher on her.
- Pharaoh twins **Anpu and Bata** won't rule apart for reasons best left to adult tables.

## The 1039 explosion
Gabiro's **Soul CREATE** ritual at Sanbi Castle (meant to build Princess Maiara a perfect husband) caused a "White Hole event" that killed Gabiro and bound a dying soul from another world into a new body (Harry). Rumors in the 1040s range from "a lab accident" to "a royal cover-up". Gabiro's research notes and Eori's nari gel recipes (Mind Eraser #37, Smooth Skin, Moist Breath) are prizes for many factions.
