# Vraki West March — DM Guide

A **West Marches** style D&D 5e campaign set in the world of #HusbandOutWorld. It grew out of the original Alpha wagon-escort one-shot (now kept here as the [Alpha One-Shot](Alpha%20One-Shot%20-%20Wagon%20Escort.md)), expanded into an open table.

The campaign **takes place in the world of the light novels but doesn't follow their story**. Canon characters are NPCs and rumors at most. By default it's the **1040s A.G.** — the DM decides the exact year and who's alive.

## The pitch for players
You're a badge-carrying **Pearl adventurer** working out of a Forway **embassy**. Grab a quest off the board, sign the contract in blood, gather whoever's free tonight, and head out into a world full of walking killer trees, moody undead castles, bandits, bureaucracy and food. Climb from **Pearl to Sapphire**. Try not to die — and if you do, hope someone drags your body back before your soul dissolves, because Forway *will* revive you... and take half your pay until the debt is paid.

## How a West March runs
1. **Open table.** There's a pool of players, not a fixed party. Anyone can propose a session; whoever signs up that day is the party (3–6 is ideal).
2. **Players drive.** Before a session, the players pick a **quest** from the board (or a rumor, a ruin, a spot on the map they want to explore) and tell the DM. The DM preps that.
3. **Start and end at an embassy.** Every session starts at an embassy city (the hub is **Forway**) and should end with the party back at any embassy, or at least in a safe town. If they're still out in the wild when the session ends, the **quest deadline** keeps ticking (see [Death, Revival & Debt](05%20-%20Death,%20Revival%20&%20Debt.md)).
4. **One shared world.** Whatever a party kills, maps, angers or befriends stays that way for everyone. Keep a shared **session log** and mark traveled and explored circles on a shared copy of the **travel map**.
5. **Time is real-ish.** Track the in-world date. Two parties can't be in two places at the same in-world time; if timelines collide, the DM sorts it out generously.
6. **Rewards come from the world**: quest pay, loot, bounties, promotions, guild connections, gossip worth money.

## The big simplification: travel by circles
No kilometers, no Egua speeds, no load percentages. The [travel map](../Maps) has circles drawn along the routes connecting places. **Count the circles to the destination. Each circle represents 1, 2 or 3 days — the DM chooses** (Fast, Standard, Slow) to make the journey shorter or longer. Details in [Travel by Circles](01%20-%20Travel%20by%20Circles.md).

## Changes from the original Alpha campaign
- Travel uses **circles and a pace knob** instead of distances, Egua speeds and load math.
- The wagon escort is now **one quest type** (Cargo Protection) among many; the four daily **shifts** are kept.
- Combat uses normal **initiative** (the Alpha text said turns go by AC).
- Added rules for soul energy, sigil rings, nari gels, revival debt, ranks, quests, events, factions and dangerous places.
- Pre-gens cleaned up: **Hiromi** is now a Darfaries brawler (matching the lore), and his old interrogator kit moved to a new pre-gen, **Takeo**. Every sheet lists its people, interface and Soul Points.
- Fauna and beast stat blocks gained regions, a few fixes ("Monica" → Monika), and new entries (Shapeshifter, Trunk People, Nari Worm, Flinga, Bruntos, White Roundies, Hash template), plus guards and bandits.

## For DMs
Whatever makes the game more fun — do it. Turn the circle knob, ignore a rule, invent a village. If you have a proposal, send a PR. If you run a game, tell us — we love pics!
