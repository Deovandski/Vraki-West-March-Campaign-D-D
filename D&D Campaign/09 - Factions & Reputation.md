# Factions & Reputation

Track the party's (or each character's) reputation with factions from **−3 to +3**. Start at 0. Shared reputation is part of the shared world: one party's choices affect everyone.

| Rep | Meaning |
|---|---|
| −3 | Kill on sight / hunted |
| −2 | Hostile; prices doubled; ambushes |
| −1 | Distrusted |
| 0 | Unknown |
| +1 | Friendly; small favors, fair prices |
| +2 | Trusted; secret quests, safe houses, discounts |
| +3 | Family; they'll risk a lot for you |

## Gaining and losing
+1 for a completed job or a big favor for them; −1 for hurting their interests; −2 for killing members or exposing their secrets. One step per session at most.

## Guilds as factions
Joining a guild (or founding one, 1 sapphire) gives the party a home, shared storage, inheritance rules and a family name. Guild reputation can be tracked like a faction.
