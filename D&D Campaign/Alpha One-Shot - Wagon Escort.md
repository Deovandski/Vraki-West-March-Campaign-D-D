# Alpha One-Shot: Wagon Escort

The campaign involves **2–6 players** working as adventurers protecting a wagon from one place to another. More of a one‑shot type of campaign.

The original #HusbandOutWorld Alpha campaign, kept here for quick games. For the full open-table campaign, see the [DM Guide](README.md); this one-shot works as a single **Cargo Protection** quest in the West March too.

---

## Daily Structure

Each day is split into chunks:

### **Graveyard (12am–6am)**
- Players decide who stays awake, if anyone.
- DM rolls dice for events.

### **Morning (6am–12pm)**
- Players decide their actions.
- DM rolls dice for events (or not).

### **Afternoon (12pm–6pm)**
- Players decide their actions.
- DM rolls dice for events (or not).

### **Night (6pm–12am)**
- Players decide their actions.
- DM rolls dice for events.

---

## Player Decisions

Players can create their own characters, or use an existing adventurer. See [Adventurers](Adventurers) for pre-made characters, or [Making an Adventurer](02%20-%20Making%20an%20Adventurer.md).

For each time chunk, players decide:

1. **Stay for rest** — repair, hunt for good dinner, barter, flirt, steal, etc.  
   If in town or staying with other wagons, social actions become available. Prep Nari gels, etc.

2. **Travel** — keep the wagon moving, use companions (if they have them) to check for danger, let the Eguas graze, etc.

3. **Roles** — who stays awake, who actively protects, who rests.

4. **Combat** — roll initiative as usual.

## DM Decisions

1. Events, enemies for event types, etc. Possible events:
###### *Ambush from Fauna or beast. Bagarisa swarm, etc
###### *Ambush from bandits
###### *Find corpses (adventurers, other wagon, etc.)
###### *Broken wagon.
###### *Nothing.

2. Rewards.  
3. Punishment (if caught by guards or reported by one of the people in the wagon or someone else.)

## Campaign Duration: travel by circles

No need to calculate distances, Egua speeds or loads. The travel map has **circles drawn along the routes connecting places**.

1. **Count the circles** from point A to point B along the map.
2. **Each circle represents 1, 2 or 3 days of travel — the DM chooses**, to make the trip shorter or longer:
   - **1 day (Fast)** — good roads, light loads, or the DM wants to get to the action.
   - **2 days (Standard)** — a normal wagon on roads and trails.
   - **3 days (Slow)** — heavy wagon, off-road, swamp, desert, mountains, bad weather, or the DM wants the journey to *be* the adventure.
3. **Days × 4 shifts** = how many time chunks you play. Roll events per shift, or once per circle to speed things up. Assuming ~5 minutes of playtime per shift, each day of travel is ~20 minutes of play.

**Eguas** graze during the Morning and Night and eat ~20 kg of grass, hay or Carbey a day — but there's no speed or load math. Pushing them harder is a good reason for the DM to pick a faster pace (with Con saves for everyone and cranky Eguas); bad terrain is a good reason for a slower one.

The original metadata map with kilometers lives in the [#HusbandOutWorld repo](https://github.com/Deovandski/HusbandOutWorld/tree/main/Illustrations/World%20Map) for the lore-curious. Full travel rules: [Travel by Circles](01%20-%20Travel%20by%20Circles.md).
