# Beast Stat Blocks

Ported from the original Alpha campaign's `Beasts Stats.md`, with regions added and new entries. Light-weight blocks: fill in ability scores from a similar 5e monster of the same CR when you need them. "Soul Energy" damage reduces the target's **Soul Points** (SP); below 0 SP a creature gains exhaustion instead.

## Hash (template) — any CR, Hifuna
Apply to any creature that lived in Hifuna: **+2 CR-ish**. Double HP; +2 to attack and damage rolls; Intelligence halved; always hostile; usually sterile. Its special abilities are stronger (+1d6 damage, +2 save DCs). Meat is irradiated (eating it = 1 Exposure). Proof of kill: the head.

## Sand Worm (CR 5) — Desert
AC 14 · HP 110 · burrow 30 ft
- **Harden Body.** Bonus action: +2 AC until its next turn.
- **Acid Spray (Recharge 5–6).** 15-ft cone, 4d10 acid, DC 14 Dex half.
- **Burrow Cloak.** Invisible while underground; Saynas detect it.
- **Enlarge (1/day).** Doubles size: reach +5 ft, damage +1d6.
- **Bite.** +7 to hit, 3d8+4 piercing.
- *Loot:* chitin (worth 2 aquamarines from an adult), gamey meat.

## Walking Flesh (CR 4) — Bahyum, Buhraies Plains
AC 13 · HP 95
- **Slow Regeneration.** Regains 5 HP per round unless it took fire or radiant damage.
- **Halted Decomposition.** Immune to poison and disease.
- **Hive Mind.** Can't be surprised while another Walking undead is within 60 ft.
- **Decompose Touch.** +6 to hit, 2d8+4 necrotic.
- **Soul Drain (Recharge 6).** One creature within 5 ft: DC 14 Con save or lose 1d4 SP.

## Walking Skin (CR 3) — Bahyum, Buhraies Plains
AC 12 · HP 70
- **Slow Regeneration.** 3 HP per round unless it took radiant damage.
- **Decompose Strike.** +5 to hit, 1d8+3 necrotic.
- **Soul Theft.** On a hit, the target loses 1 SP.
- **Mindless Swarm.** Advantage on attacks while an ally is adjacent to the target.

## Walking Flathead (CR 7) — Bahyum
AC 17 · HP 150
- **Slow Regeneration.** 10 HP per round unless it took radiant damage.
- **Soul Sight.** Sees invisible and soul-based creatures within 120 ft.
- **Flathead Slam.** +8 to hit, 2d10+5 bludgeoning.
- **Worm Brood.** On a critical hit, the target is infested: DC 15 Con save each long rest or lose 1 max HP until a Tier II Body RESTORE.
- **Unkillable.** When reduced to 0 HP, DC 15 Con save to drop to 1 HP instead (not if the damage was radiant).
- *Loot:* claws (weapon material), tongue (Walking Dead gel). A whole corpse sells for **5 sapphires**.

## Wood People (CR 3) — Buhraies Plains, Tolerideme
AC 14 · HP 80 · speed 20 ft
- **Vine Strike (Fast Strike).** Reach 15 ft, +6 to hit, 2d8+4 piercing.
- **Rooted Defense.** +2 AC if it hasn't moved this turn.
- **Growth Burst.** Bonus action: +10 ft reach until its next turn.
- **Mindless Rampage.** Must attack the nearest creature.
- **Fost Weakness.** A fost stone jammed into its crown (attack vs AC 14, or automatic while it's grappled or prone) **paralyzes** it until removed. It's drawn to fost stones within 60 ft.
- *Loot:* red wood, rubbery vines, pink sap, a head-sized Wood People Yabuca.

## Hifuna People (CR 6) — Hifuna
AC 16 · HP 120
- **Fast Strike.** Reach 15 ft, +7 to hit, 2d6+4 slashing; two attacks.
- **Sonic Boom (Recharge 5–6).** 30-ft cone, 3d10 thunder, DC 15 Con half.
- **Razor Edges.** Crits deal +2d6 slashing.
- **Spike Launch.** Ranged 60 ft, +7 to hit, 2d8 piercing.
- **Exploding Branches (1/day).** Thrown 60 ft: 4d8 fire in a 10-ft radius, DC 15 Dex half.
- **Soul Leaves.** Senses soul energy within 60 ft. Fost stone paralysis works, but the attack is against AC 16.

## Teefyro (CR 4) — Hifuna, near Tastyleaf
AC 13 · HP 65
- **Teeth Launch.** Ranged 30 ft, +6 to hit, 2d8+4 piercing.
- **Random Mutation (Recharge 5–6).** Gains one for 1 minute: +2 AC · +2d6 damage · +20 ft speed · resistance to a random damage type.
- **Body Explosion.** When reduced to 0 HP, explodes: 4d6 fire in 15 ft, DC 13 Dex half. Leaves a crater.

## Koalin (CR 1) — eastern grasslands
AC 12 · HP 40
- **Dread Aura.** Creatures that start their turn within 30 ft: DC 12 Wis save or frightened until the start of their next turn.
- **Bigger Looking.** Illusion: appears Large.
- **Hypnosis (Recharge 6).** One creature within 60 ft: DC 12 Wis save or incapacitated for 1 round.
- **Mind Crush (if cornered).** DC 13 Wis save or 3d6 psychic; weak-minded NPCs may flee or worse.
- *Loot:* meat (tastes like pork), hide. Brain, eyes and mustache are **illegal** to carry.

## Sweet Sting (CR 2) — Sweetlake, west of Blood
AC 14 · HP 55 · fly 40 ft
- **Pincher Shot.** Ranged 60 ft, +6 to hit, 3d6+3 piercing (black flame on the pincers is the tell).
- **Poison Spit (Recharge 5–6).** One target within 60 ft automatically takes 4d10 poison. "Sure as a spitting Sweet Sting."
- **Pincher Copy.** Reaction: a spectral pincer duplicate gives +2 AC for 1 round.
- **Costume Blind.** Ignores creatures in a convincing Sweet Sting costume.

## Crazed Muckman (CR 3) — Blood, Freeish
AC 13 · HP 75
- **Slam.** +6 to hit, 2d8+4 bludgeoning.
- **Mutate (Recharge 5–6).** Gains one: +2 AC · +2d6 damage · +20 ft speed · resistance to a random type.
- **Unstable Form.** When hit by a critical hit, splits into two smaller Muckmen (15 HP each).
- Not aggressive unless provoked or hungry.

## Sucker Lady (CR 3) — forests near towns, Hifuna
AC 14 · HP 60
- **Stalker.** Picks one target and follows them for 1d3 nights (DC 15 Perception to notice). Advantage on attacks against isolated targets.
- **Soul Drain Grapple.** +6 to hit, 1d8+4 bludgeoning, grappled (escape DC 14).
- **Pin & Feed.** A grappled target takes 2d10 necrotic each round and loses 1 SP.
- **Fost Fear.** Won't approach a lit fost lamp (DC 14 Wis save to try). Distractible by "something finger-shaped".

## Big Winga (CR 4) — Wingarest, rare
AC 15 · HP 95 · fly 80 ft
- **Aerial Strike.** +7 to hit, 2d8+4 slashing.
- **Soul-Enhanced Screech (Recharge 5–6).** 3d10 thunder in 30 ft, DC 15 Con half.
- **Dive Bomb.** If it flies 20 ft straight toward the target, +2d6 damage.
- **Air Shield.** Reaction: +3 AC against one ranged attack.
- **Sky Hunter.** Advantage on sight Perception; senses living creatures within 120 ft.

## Shapeshifter (CR 4) — west of Blood
AC 12 · HP 70 · amorphous
- **Mimic.** Assumes the form and voice of a Medium or smaller creature it has touched (DC 15 Insight to spot something off).
- **Engulf.** +5 to hit, 2d6+3 bludgeoning and grappled; 2d6 acid each turn while grappled.
- **Split.** When it drops below half HP, it can split into two (each with half the remaining HP).
- *Loot:* jelly blob in a jar (1 aquamarine to scientists).

## Trunk People (CR 1) — Freeish forests, Woodland
AC 13 · HP 22 · Small
- **Four Flame Arms.** Two attacks, +4 to hit, 1d6 fire.
- **Blinding Flash (Recharge 5–6).** 20-ft radius, DC 12 Con save or blinded 1 round.
- **Deaf, Talks in Light.** Can't hear; communicates with colors.
- In Freeish they're street lights and guards; groups of 6+ can set a village on fire.

## Flinga (CR 6) — the skies
AC 15 · HP 110 · fly 120 ft
- **Stunning Scream (Recharge 5–6).** 60-ft cone, DC 15 Con save or stunned 1 round.
- **Gust Stream.** 60-ft line, DC 15 Str save or pushed 30 ft and knocked prone.
- **Climb Away.** When damaged, it flies straight up out of reach.
- Untamable. Its eggs never hatch for humans.

## Nari Worm (CR 8, but peaceful) — Nari Caverns
AC 18 · HP 200 · burrow 40 ft
- **Friendly.** Never attacks first. Harming one is a Hifuna-exile crime.
- **Harden Shell / Acid Gut.** If attacked: +2 AC, and 4d10 acid in a 20-ft line (DC 16 Dex half).
- **Quake.** Each round it's angry, the cavern quakes (DC 13 Dex or 2d6 bludgeoning, prone).

## White Roundies (CR 1) — Whiteland, winter shores
AC 12 · HP 30 · swim 50 ft
- **Purr.** Creatures that pet one gain 1 temporary HP (once per day).
- **Sound Blast (Recharge 6).** 15-ft radius, DC 12 Con save or deafened 1 minute (used to stun fish).
- **Trade.** Give it good food: d6 → 1–2 half-eaten fish, 3–4 shiny junk, 5 a pearl, 6 a gold nugget (1 amethyst).

## Bruntos (CR ½) — Endlessfog, Freeish (feral)
AC 12 · HP 19 · climb 30 ft
- **Porter.** Carries as much as a mule over rough terrain without slowing the party.
- **Fist.** +4 to hit, 1d6+2 bludgeoning.
- Protected on Endlessfog: harming one there makes the locals violent.

## Monica Swarm (CR 2) — lower plains
AC 12 · HP 30 · swarm, fly 30 ft
- **Strip Flesh.** Swarm attack: 2d6 piercing to creatures in its space; a corpse is stripped in hours.
- **Storm Warning.** A blue wall of them means a storm within the day.
