**Cinika (CR 0)** — AC 11 · HP 5 · fly 30 ft
Paralyzing Dust: DC 10 Con save or paralyzed 1 round. Weather Sense: detects pressure, humidity and temperature changes. Swarm Instinct: advantage on attacks while adjacent to another Cinika. Favors licking Wozefaries and Sorefaries sweat.

**Culuim (CR 1)** — AC 12 · HP 10
Heart Flip: switches Walking Heart modes (+2 to one ability, −2 to another, DM's choice). Blood Surge: +2 AC for 1 round. Contaminated Flesh: creatures that bite it make a DC 12 Con save or are poisoned.

**Egua (CR 1)** — AC 13 · HP 60 · speed 40 ft
Water Walk: walks on water. Dirt Repel: ignores difficult terrain from mud or sand. Leg Morph: bonus action, +10 ft speed or +2 AC. Intestine Ejection (Recharge 5–6): 10-ft fear aura for 1 round (DC 12 Wis). Kick: +5, 2d6+3 bludgeoning. Friendly; hurting one makes it violent.

**Legunia (CR 2)** — AC 12 · HP 20 · speed 60 ft
High Jump: 30 ft vertical. Super Speed: Dash as a bonus action. Desert Sense: senses dangerous desert conditions within 1 mile (its hump shrinks).

**Lansha (CR 3)** — AC 14 · HP 85
Gravity Down: DC 14 Str save or knocked prone. Oil Up: attackers have disadvantage for 1 round. Sour Gland Burst (Recharge 5–6): 2d10 acid in a 10-ft cone. Timid alone, territorial in herds.

**Monika (CR 0)** — AC 11 · HP 5 · fly 30 ft
Soul Sense: detects soul energy sources. Scent Vision: sees smells. Corpse Finder: advantage on Perception to find corpses (prefers high-SE bodies — dead adventurers).

**Winga / Pink Winga (CR 1)** — AC 13 · HP 30 · fly 60 ft
Life Sense: detects living creatures within 60 ft. Air Shield (Recharge 5–6): +2 AC for 1 round. Shriek: DC 12 Con save or deafened 1 round. Diamond Beak: +4, 1d6+2 piercing (ignores resistance). Pink Wingas have advantage on Stealth. Killing a Forway courier Winga is a crime.

**Wooly (CR 1)** — AC 12 · HP 50
Reverberate Voice: DC 12 Con save or deafened. Light Burst: bright light 30 ft for 1 minute. Mountain Stance: advantage against being pushed. Its death scream carries for kilometers (attracts predators).

**Barina (CR 2)** — AC 13 · HP 18 · swim 30 ft
Toxic Tail Burst (Recharge 5–6): 2d8 poison in a 10-ft radius. Cloak: invisible while submerged. Gas Diffusal: 15-ft poison cloud for 1 round. Back Spikes: creatures that grapple it take 1d4 piercing.

**Crocus (CR 2)** — AC 14 · HP 45 · swim 30 ft
Spike Burst (Recharge 5–6): 2d6 piercing in a 10-ft radius. Harden Skin: +2 AC for 1 round. Light Bend: invisible while motionless (can stay still for hours). Bite: +5, 2d6+3 piercing, grappled.

**Fatupari (CR 2)** — AC 13 · HP 40
Launch: moves 40 ft in a line; its next attack deals +1d6. Air Buoyancy: hovers for 1 round. Webbing Shield: while its pink webbing is intact, resistance to spell damage (attack the webbing, AC 13, 10 HP, to tear it). Bite: +5, 1d8+3, grappled.

**Fihi (CR 3)** — AC 14 · HP 55 · swim 40 ft
Elemental Absorb: reduces elemental damage by 1d6. Elemental Release (Recharge 5–6): 3d10 of the last element it absorbed (lightning if none) in a 15-ft cone. Shock Skin: melee attackers take 1d6 lightning. *Lake giants:* double HP; swallow a Medium creature on a crit.

**Sanbi (CR 1)** — AC 12 · HP 25 · swim 50 ft
Sonic Pulse: DC 12 Con save or migraine (disadvantage on attacks for 1 minute). Fake Prey: sheds lip/tail decoys; attackers have disadvantage. Tail Stretch: extends its tail 10 ft to avoid melee. Landing one: DC 15 Strength (Athletics), or two successes out of three for a weaker fisher.

**Ptaina (CR 2)** — AC 13 · HP 35 · swim 40 ft
Ultrasonic Burst (Recharge 5–6): 3d8 thunder underwater, 1d8 on land, 20-ft radius. Compass Distortion: creatures within 20 ft have disadvantage on Survival. Head Glow: creatures within 5 ft make a DC 12 Wis save or frightened 1 round.

**Sayna (CR 1)** — AC 13 · HP 28
Distant Hearing: hears up to 1 mile. Cloak Detection: detects invisible creatures and burrowing Sand Worms within 20 ft. Blood Shield (self-defense): gains 5 temporary HP when hit. Goofy: needs training to use skills on command. "Pohw-pohhhww!"

**Wozen (CR 2)** — AC 14 · HP 40 · speed 40 ft
Super Smell: advantage on tracking. Sound Bounce: makes no sound while moving. Light Bounce: +2 Stealth in dim light. Super Jump: 20-ft leaps. Jaw Clench: +5, 2d6+3 piercing, grappled (escape DC 13). Nocturnal; loves Bagarisa — and humans.

**Bagarisa (CR 2)** — AC 14 · HP 40
**Two Hearts:** the first time it drops to 0 HP it drops to 1 HP instead, unless the hit was a critical (you hit both hearts). Flaming Tusks: +5, 2d6+3 fire. Fireball Gust (Recharge 5–6): 3d10 fire in a 15-ft cone, DC 13 Dex half. Blood Heal: regains 5 HP when it deals fire damage. Musk: smells like shrimp brine; anyone who butchers one smells of it (Wozens track the smell).

**Fanchaki (CR 1)** — AC 12 · HP 20
Rapid Breed: once per day, 1d4 chicks appear (no actions). Peck Flurry: two attacks, +4, 1d4+2. Panic Dash: when frightened, moves 40 ft without provoking. Fake Body: the first attack against it each round has disadvantage.

**Soreno (CR 1)** — AC 12 · HP 18
Mimicry: imitates sounds perfectly. Blink Dash: bonus action, move 5 ft without provoking. Cloak: advantage on Stealth (heat and sight). Pest Swarm: advantage on attacks while adjacent to another Soreno. Long-Distance Squeak: a trained one relays a message up to 1 mile.

**Yuyta (CR ½)** — AC 11 · HP 12
Hop Strike: +3, 1d4+1. Harvest Sense: detects crop quality within 100 ft. Wall Sight: whiskers sense objects through 1 ft of wall or dirt. Group Scatter: when one is damaged, all Yuytas within 20 ft move 10 ft away. Screams like a dying human.
