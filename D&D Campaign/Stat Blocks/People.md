# People Stat Blocks

Guards, bandits and specialists. Built on 5e NPC blocks with Vraki gear. All carry an embassy badge unless noted.

## Forway Guard (CR 1) — use *Guard* + changes
AC 17 (shiny plate-ish armor) · HP 25 · speed 25 ft
- **Crossbow.** +4, 1d8+2 piercing, range 80/320.
- **Pack Tactics.** Advantage on attacks while an ally is within 5 ft of the target. Always in groups of 3+.
- **Superiority Complex.** If an enemy looked weak last turn (took the Dodge action, retreated, or failed a check), the guard has disadvantage on its next Wisdom (Insight) check and attack against that enemy.
- **Black Honey Pot (1/day).** When reduced to 0 HP, an adjacent guard can use an action to revive it at 1 HP. Steal the pot (leg pocket) to stop it.

## Forway Captain (CR 5) — use *Veteran*
Same armor as a soldier plus a **fancy cap**. Adds: **Entangle Ring** (all allies within 120 ft hear its orders), **Body RESTORE ring** (Tier II, 2/day), authority over embassy grounds.

## Big Winga Guard (CR 4)
A Forway guard in Big Winga gel: use **Big Winga** stats, but Intelligence 10, can understand Vraki, and patrols embassies.

## Sanbi Guard (CR 1) — use *Scout*
AC 13 · HP 22
- **Longbow.** +4, 1d8+2 piercing; arrows may carry a nari gel (Body Paralysis II or Sleep Inducer I).
- **Everything Is a Weapon.** Improvised weapons count as proficient (1d6).
- **Nari Bomb (1/day).** Smoke (heavily obscured 20 ft) — then they either attack or leave.

## Tolerideme Guard (CR 2) — use *Guard*, but…
AC 16 (gauntlets + padding) · HP 30 · speed 25 ft
- **Gauntlet.** +4, 1d8+2 bludgeoning.
- **Elemental Ball (slow).** Takes a full turn to charge (it's obvious); next turn ranged 60 ft, +5, 3d8 cold/fire/lightning/thunder. Only cast in packs of 4+.
- **Never Negotiates.** Immune to Persuasion attempts to bargain.

## Gandew Guard (CR 3) — use *Thug* with chitin
AC 18 (chitin) · HP 45
- **Maul.** +6, 2d6+4 bludgeoning; on a hit, DC 13 Str save or knocked prone.
- **Alone, Silent.** Helmet locks the tusks; can't speak while wearing it. Advantage on saves vs. fear.

## Freeish Guard (CR 1)
AC 14 (bone-wood) · HP 22
- **Sleeve Sword.** +5, 1d6+3; can draw and attack as one action (surprise).
- **Hallucinogen Reflexes.** Advantage on initiative and Dex saves.
- **Nari Bomb Dash.** Throws smoke, then moves 30 ft without provoking.

## Bandit (CR ⅛) / Bandit Captain (CR 2)
Use the 5e blocks. Vraki flavors:
- **Blue Wozens:** blue Wozen badge; prefer robbery to killing ("donation"). 
- **Beer Balls:** drunk; disadvantage on Wis saves; fight fiercely for beer.
- **Wood People bandits:** carry fost stones to herd and steer Wood People (DC 12 to steer one 30 ft).
- **Fallen Tree:** ex-adventurers — use *Veteran*/*Spy*; hate embassy staff.
- **Black Magina:** never kill; Sleep Inducer darts (+4, DC 13 Con or unconscious 1 minute).
- **Bagarisa Brothers:** use *Berserker*; secret tusk handshake.

## Kozaku Forway, Blue Wozens Boss (CR 9)
Mixed Human–Darfaries; red Walking Heart (undead).
AC 16 · HP 170 · speed 40 ft
- **Undead.** No breathing, eating or sleep; immune to poison; can't be revived.
- **Red Heart Rage.** Multiattack: three punches, +9, 2d10+6 bludgeoning. On a crit, the target's head is… gone (DC 15 Con save to survive at 0 HP instead).
- **Unstoppable.** Advantage on Str checks and saves. Red flame bursts from his chest when angry.
- **Burnout.** If he uses Multiattack 3 turns in a row, he takes 2d10 necrotic each extra turn (his soul is burning). Someone must stop him — or let him burn out.

## The Stinger (CR 5) — Morika in Sweet Sting chitin
AC 17 · HP 85
- **Stinger Slash.** Two attacks, +7, 1d8+4 slashing.
- **Gore Charge.** Moves 20 ft straight and attacks: +7, 2d6+4 piercing; prone on a hit (DC 14 Str).
- **Armor Spike Burst (Recharge 6).** 2d6 piercing to all adjacent.
- **Urban Legend.** Commoners who see it make a DC 12 Wis save or flee. Out of the armor he's mute, polite and anxious.

## Red Winga Agent (CR 2) — use *Spy*
Perfect cover identity (maid, florist, courtesan); Fake Skin gel; poisoned hairpin (Bleed Out I). "We are here to please you."

## Soul Energy Scientist (CR 2) — use *Mage* (lower slots)
Sigil rings for four spells; a satchel of three random nari gels; lab notes worth 1 aquamarine to the right buyer; terrible people skills.

## Embassy Clerk (CR 0) — use *Commoner*
Knows every quest, every deadline and everyone's debt. Bribable with gossip.
