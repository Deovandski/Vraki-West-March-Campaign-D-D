# Dangerous Places

Special rules for the places that make West March parties famous — or dead.

## Bahyum Castle — the Castle Mood
The castle is alive, moody, and still running Buhraies experiments. Bahyum village posts **"Today's Castle Mood"** every morning.

**Mood track (1–6).** At the start of each in-world day, roll a d6: on a 1 the mood drops by 1; on a 6 it rises by 1. Parties fighting inside raise it by 1 each day they spend there.

| Mood | Name | Inside the castle | Outside |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sleepy | Undead idle; doors open; a **new room** appears 1-in-6 | Quiet |
| 2 | Grumpy | Patrols of Walking Skin | Quiet |
| 3 | Restless | Walking Flesh hunt intruders; traps arm | Stragglers on the plains |
| 4 | Angry | Rooms rearrange; a Walking Flathead wakes | Walking Skin packs raid nearby farms |
| 5 | Furious | Two Flatheads; soul-drain fog (1 SP per hour) | Embassy posts an Aquamarine quest |
| 6 | **Rampage** | Everything awake | Waves of undead pour out with **five commanders** (Flatheads). **Kingdom Alert**: all adventurers called. Kill the commanders to drop the mood to 3 |

**Loot:** Buhraies research notes, Walking Heart stones (illegal), soul wood, black wood, ancient sigil rings (old-style rings are collectibles), and the occasional thing that should never leave.
**New rooms** are a great excuse for one-shot dungeons.

## Hifuna — exposure
Irradiated land. Everything mutates.
- **Each day** in Hifuna (or each hour near a Walking Heart deposit), make a **DC 12 Constitution save** (DC 15 deep inside). On a failure, gain 1 **Exposure**.
- Exposure 1: nausea (disadvantage on Con checks) · 2: hair falls out, −1 max SP · 3: you start to become **Hash** — +2 Strength, −2 Intelligence, you look wrong · 4: a full Hash mutation (DM and player design it; the character may need to retire as an NPC monster).
- **Long rest outside Hifuna** removes 1 Exposure. A Tier III Nari Refresher or Body RESTORE (Tier III) removes all but the Hash change at 3+.
- **Food** grown or hunted in Hifuna gives 1 Exposure when eaten.
- **Walking Heart stones** grow here. A character who implants one (via a scientist willing to do it) becomes undead: no eating/breathing/sleep, recovers SP only through strong emotion or stabbing nari stones into the chest, takes the stone's color powers (+2 to one ability, −2 to another, per the color table in the World Lore) and its emotional flaw. **Can't be revived.** Forway treats them as soul dead and wanted.

## The Desert (Gandew, Flatland, Sundeath)
- Each day without water: DC 12 Con save or 1 exhaustion (DC 15 in Paulin).
- Nights are freezing: no warm gear → DC 10 Con save or 1 exhaustion.
- **Sand Worms:** a Sayna or Wozen Smell gel spots them; otherwise DC 15 Perception.
- Tunnels between Gandew and Sundeath are full of beasts — go overland.
- **Gandew law:** any dispute can become a duel. Refuse and you lose face — and in Gandew, face matters.
- **Sundeath law:** guilty until proven innocent. Have friends.

## The Buhraies Plains
- Wood People groves wander. A **fost stone** jammed into a crown (melee attack vs AC 14, or automatic against a grappled tree) paralyzes one.
- The plateau edge is a 2 km cliff. Waterfall's elevators are the safe way up.
- **Lukewarm Lake:** Fihi big enough to drag a person under (use Fihi stats ×2 HP, swallow on a crit).

## Nari Caverns (under Blood)
- **Noise:** every loud action (combat, explosions, shouting) — roll a d6; on a 1 the worms stir and the cavern **quakes** (DC 13 Dex save or 2d6 bludgeoning and knocked prone).
- **Harming a Nari Worm or its eggs** is a crime punishable by exile to Hifuna. The Nari Extraction Coordination Office in Blood investigates every death.
- The **Deep Hole Chamber** is rumored to hold the Queen Nari Worm and raw nari deposits.

## Sweetlake Nest
- Without a Sweet Sting costume (Blood and Sweetlake tailors, 2 amethysts), every hour near the nest: 1d4 Sweet Sting workers attack.
- With one: DC 12 Charisma (Performance) every hour to keep acting like a bee.

## Swamps (Cruro lands, Sanbi–Tastyleaf)
- Slow pace; Crocus invisible in still water (DC 14 Perception).
- **Cruros barter** — they aren't citizens, so it's legal. Bring Wooly jerky.
- Tribe leaders challenge strangers who stare too long. Losing badly can mean an unwanted "marriage" to a leader's daughter.

## Ganyrami Ruins
- No guards, no badges, no reports. Contracts are enforced by whoever's bigger.
- Asking about anyone's past: DC 15 Persuasion or the conversation (and maybe the welcome) ends.

## Triangle City
- Everyone follows **the Triangle Way**: bathroom breaks at the same time (a gong tells you when), triangle-cut clothes, no pets. Non-humans wear a triangle amulet.
- Break the Way publicly: the Triangle Lovers escort you out — or somewhere worse.

## Fuhrair
- Altitude ~9 km: −20 °C or colder. Without cold-weather gear, DC 13 Con save each hour or 1 exhaustion. Winga Skin and Wooly Skin gels help a lot.
- Doorless snow houses connect by tunnels. Old Fuhraies traps. Hidden Forway science stations. Something kept prisoner in the dark (see DM Secrets).
