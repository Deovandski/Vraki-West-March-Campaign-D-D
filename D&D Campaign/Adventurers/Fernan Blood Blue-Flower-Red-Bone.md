# Fernan Blood Blue-Flower-Red-Bone — Level 3 Fighter (Arcane Archer-lite)
**People:** "Pure" human · **Class:** Fighter · **Background:** Sapphire Hero (young) · **Alignment:** Chaotic Good · **Rank:** Pearl
**Interface:** none (can't use sigil rings) · **Soul Points:** 4 (+1 extra on each long rest: Deep Soul)

> **Canon note:** in the lore, Fernan is a famous Sapphire who "died in Hifuna". Play him as his **young self** in a flashback/one-shot, as a look-alike, or with your table's blessing to bend canon. The West March doesn't follow the novels, so it's your call.

**AC** 16 · **HP** 32 · **Speed** 30 ft
**STR** 14 · **DEX** 18 · **CON** 14 · **INT** 11 · **WIS** 13 · **CHA** 16

### Features & Traits
- **Born Hero.** Advantage on Persuasion checks with civilians.
- **Selfless Defender.** When an ally within 5 ft is hit, Fernan can use his reaction to impose disadvantage on the attack (1/day).
- **Romantic Fool.** Disadvantage on Insight checks involving attractive Wozefaries with long hair.
- **Archer's Pride.** +1 damage with bows.

### Actions
- **Longbow.** +7 to hit, 1d8+5 piercing.
- **Shortsword.** +6 to hit, 1d6+4 slashing.
- **Burst Arrow (2/day, or 1 SP).** 1d6 force in a 5-ft radius on impact.
- **Seeking Arrow (1/day, or 2 SP).** Automatically hits for 1d8+4.

**Skills:** Athletics +4, Perception +3, Persuasion +5, Acrobatics +6
**Equipment:** Masterwork bow (chitin and bone wood, Winga-beak blades for close quarters), quiver, jerky, travel cloak, a carved statue of Akemi, badge.
