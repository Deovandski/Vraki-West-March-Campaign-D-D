# July Forway Tea-Drinkers — Level 3 Monk (CQC Specialist)
**People:** Human · **Class:** Monk · **Background:** Tea-Drinkers Guild Fighter · **Alignment:** Chaotic Good · **Rank:** Pearl
**Interface:** Darfaries · **Soul Points:** 3

**AC** 15 · **HP** 27 · **Speed** 40 ft
**STR** 12 · **DEX** 16 · **CON** 12 · **INT** 11 · **WIS** 14 · **CHA** 16

### Features & Traits
- **Voracious Charmer.** Advantage on Persuasion checks with men.
- **Nari Gel Trauma.** If someone forces a nari gel on her, she goes berserk for 1 round (attacks them, advantage, can't be calmed). She warns first.
- **Protective of Mugi.** +2 to all rolls while defending her Sayna, Mugi.
- **CQC Master.** Unarmed strikes deal 1d6 instead of 1d4.

### Monk Features
- **Martial Arts (1d6).**
- **Ki (3 points):** Flurry of Blows, Step of the Wind, Patient Defense.

### Actions
- **Unarmed Strike.** +5 to hit, 1d6+3 bludgeoning.
- **Grapple Flip.** On a hit, DC 12 Dex save or knocked prone.
- **Pressure-Point Burst (1 Ki).** 2d6 psychic, DC 12 Con save for half.

### Companion: Mugi (Sayna)
Use the Sayna stat block (Fauna). Hears up to a mile, spots invisible creatures and Sand Worms. Cuddly. Smarter than a cat.

**Skills:** Acrobatics +5, Persuasion +5, Insight +4, Performance +5
**Equipment:** Tea-Drinkers uniform, hand wraps, hidden dagger, Mugi's grooming kit, badge.
**Hook:** Don't pry into her past. She'll tell you once. Then she won't.
