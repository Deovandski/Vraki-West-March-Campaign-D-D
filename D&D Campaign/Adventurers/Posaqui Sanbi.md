# Posaqui Sanbi — Level 3 Bard (Glamour)
**People:** Human · **Class:** Bard (College of Glamour) · **Background:** Information Broker · **Alignment:** Neutral · **Rank:** Pearl
**Interface:** Darfaries · **Soul Points:** 3

**AC** 14 · **HP** 22 · **Speed** 30 ft
**STR** 8 · **DEX** 14 · **CON** 12 · **INT** 13 · **WIS** 10 · **CHA** 18

### Features & Traits
- **Grandiose Performer.** Advantage on Performance checks.
- **Narcissistic Flare.** When complimented, +1 to his next roll.
- **Beauty Rage.** If his looks are insulted, DC 12 Wis save or he lashes out.
- **Talk-Your-Way-Out.** Once per long rest, charm a humanoid for 10 minutes.
- **Friends in High Places.** Has a contract with someone powerful in Sanbi. (DM: see DM Secrets.)

### Bard Features
Bardic Inspiration (d6), Jack of All Trades, Mantle of Inspiration.

### Actions
- **Rapier.** +4 to hit, 1d8+2 piercing.
- **Cutting Words.** Reaction: subtract 1d6 from an enemy's roll.
- **Glamour Burst (Recharge 6).** 2d6 psychic, DC 14 Wis save for half.

### Spellcasting
**Spell Save DC** 14 · *Charm Person, Dissonant Whispers, Healing Word, Faerie Fire, Silent Image, Suggestion*

**Skills:** Deception +8, Performance +8, Persuasion +8, Insight +2
**Equipment:** Sparkling rapier, makeup kit, blue accessories, forged documents, a mirror, badge.
**Hook:** Avoids fights, loves blue, hates fat Toleridemians, and always knows a little too much.
