# Magi Blood Blue-Flower-Red-Bone — Level 3 Ranger
**People:** Human (strong Wozefaries blood) · **Class:** Ranger · **Background:** Guild Debtor · **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral · **Rank:** Pearl
**Interface:** Darfaries · **Soul Points:** 3

**AC** 14 · **HP** 26 · **Speed** 30 ft
**STR** 10 · **DEX** 16 · **CON** 12 · **INT** 14 · **WIS** 15 · **CHA** 11

### Features & Traits
- **Narcolepsy.** When rolling initiative, roll a d6. On a 1, Magi starts the fight asleep until an ally uses an action to wake her (or she takes damage).
- **Hunter's Instinct.** Advantage on Survival checks to track.
- **Distrustful.** Disadvantage on Persuasion checks with strangers.
- **Wozefaries Blood.** Proficiency in Acrobatics; resistance to cold damage.
- **Guild Debt.** Her family guild owes money; half her personal loot goes home to Blood.

### Actions
- **Longbow.** +6 to hit, 1d8+3 piercing.
- **Shortsword.** +5 to hit, 1d6+3 slashing.
- **Snare Trap (Recharge 6).** DC 12 Dex save or restrained.

**Spell Save DC** 13 · **Slots** 3 (1st level)
*Hunter's Mark, Absorb Elements, Goodberry*

**Skills:** Stealth +6, Survival +5, Perception +5, Investigation +4, Acrobatics +5
**Equipment:** Bow, 20 arrows, traps, camouflage cloth, pink blanket, fruit snacks, a Time Quicken jar (for staying awake), badge.
