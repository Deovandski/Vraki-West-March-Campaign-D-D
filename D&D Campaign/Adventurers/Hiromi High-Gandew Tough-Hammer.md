# Hiromi High-Gandew Tough-Hammer — Level 3 Fighter (Gandew Brawler)
**People:** Darfaries · **Class:** Fighter (Champion) · **Background:** Royal Runaway (First Prince of Gandew) · **Alignment:** Chaotic Good · **Rank:** Pearl (he never bothered to get promoted)
**Interface:** Darfaries · **Soul Points:** 5

> **Change from the original Alpha sheet:** it made Hiromi a human interrogator rogue — those traits actually belong to Takeo (see *Takeo Forway Tea-Drinkers*). This sheet matches the lore: a 232 cm Darfaries prince who'd rather drink and wander.

**AC** 16 (chitin scale) · **HP** 31 · **Speed** 30 ft
**STR** 17 · **DEX** 12 · **CON** 16 · **INT** 10 · **WIS** 11 · **CHA** 14

### Features & Traits
- **Darfaries.** Powerful Build, Tusks (1d6+3 piercing), Darfaries Veins (senses spells and weather within 30 ft), Soften the Fall (30 ft), Neutralizing Saliva (1/long rest).
- **Storyteller.** Every story stars Hiromi. Advantage on Performance checks to tell a tale in a tavern; disadvantage on Stealth when he's talking (he's always talking).
- **Round's on Me.** Spend 10 pearls in a tavern: advantage on Charisma checks there for the evening.
- **Flies Off the Handle.** If someone insults something he holds dear (Yacamim, Gandew, booze), DC 12 Wis save or he picks a fight.
- **Gandew Duelist.** Knows the Duel Rights by heart; advantage on checks to start, referee or end a Gandew-style duel.

### Fighter Features
- **Second Wind.** Bonus action: regain 1d10+3 HP (1/short rest).
- **Action Surge.** One extra action (1/short rest).
- **Improved Critical.** Crits on 19–20.

### Actions
- **Maul.** +5 to hit, 2d6+3 bludgeoning.
- **Tusks.** +5 to hit, 1d6+3 piercing.
- **Belly Shake Shove.** Shove a creature 10 ft instead of 5 (Mountain Greeting gone wrong).

**Skills:** Athletics +5, Intimidation +4, Performance +4, Survival +2
**Equipment:** Maul (his mother forged it), chitin scale armor, a quaich bowl, three bottles of something strong, picture book of the sea, badge (High-Gandew).
**Hook:** Engaged to a Sanbi princess he beat in a bar brawl. Neither of them wants the crown.
