# Morika Tolerideme — Level 3 Barbarian ("The Stinger")
**People:** Human · **Class:** Barbarian (Path of the Beast-lite) · **Background:** Unknown Wanderer · **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral · **Rank:** Pearl (on paper)
**Interface:** Darfaries · **Soul Points:** 5

**AC** 15 (Stinger chitin armor) / 15 unarmored (10 + Dex + Con) · **HP** 36 · **Speed** 30 ft
**STR** 16 · **DEX** 14 · **CON** 16 · **INT** 10 · **WIS** 12 · **CHA** 10

### Features & Traits
- **Dual Persona.** *Armor on:* bold, loud, reckless (The Stinger). *Armor off:* mute, meticulous, polite to the point of sounding robotic.
- **Blood-Soaked Wanderer.** Advantage on Intimidation while covered in gore.
- **Urban Legend.** Commoners have disadvantage to identify the Stinger as Morika.
- **Too Thin for Tolerideme.** Toleridemians mock him; he avoids home.

### Barbarian Features
- **Rage (3/day).** +2 damage; resistance to bludgeoning, piercing and slashing.
- **Unarmored Defense.** AC = 10 + Dex + Con when the armor is off.
- **Reckless Attack, Danger Sense.**

### Actions
- **Stinger Slash.** +5 to hit, 1d8+3 slashing.
- **Gore Charge (raging).** +5 to hit, 2d6+3 piercing.
- **Armor Spike Burst (Recharge 6).** 2d6 piercing to everything within 5 ft.

**Skills:** Athletics +5, Survival +3, Perception +3
**Equipment:** Stinger chitin armor (modeled on a Sweet Sting), serrated blade, blood-stained cloak, journal, a jar of black honey, badge.
**Hook:** Nobody knows the Stinger is Morika. Keep it that way.
