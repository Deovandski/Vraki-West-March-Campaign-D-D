# Takeo Forway Tea-Drinkers — Level 3 Rogue (Inquisitive)
**People:** Human (looks Darfaries — tall, big tusks — but has no Darfaries veins) · **Class:** Rogue · **Background:** Guild Muscle (Tea-Drinkers) · **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral · **Rank:** Pearl
**Interface:** Darfaries · **Soul Points:** 4

> **New pre-gen:** this sheet carries the interrogator traits that the Alpha campaign had put on Hiromi.

**AC** 14 · **HP** 27 · **Speed** 30 ft
**STR** 14 · **DEX** 16 · **CON** 14 · **INT** 12 · **WIS** 13 · **CHA** 10

### Features & Traits
- **Reserved Politeness.** Advantage on Persuasion checks when calm; disadvantage when stressed.
- **Interrogator's Edge.** Proficiency with torture tools (his finger-clipping pliers); +2 to Intimidation.
- **Not Quite Darfaries.** Immune to being charmed for 1 round (1/day). Doesn't like being asked about his veins.
- **Conflict Avoidant.** If allies argue, DC 10 Wis save or he leaves the scene for 1 round ("We finished the quest. We celebrated. Now it's time for me to say goodbye.").
- **Hifuna Yabuca Habit.** Once per long rest he can eat a Hifuna fruit for +2 Strength for 1 hour. He also gains 1 Exposure (see Dangerous Places).

### Rogue Features
- **Sneak Attack (2d6).**
- **Cunning Action.** Dash, Disengage or Hide as a bonus action.
- **Insightful Fighting.** Bonus action: Insight vs. the target's Deception to get Sneak Attack without advantage.

### Actions
- **Dagger.** +5 to hit, 1d4+3 piercing.
- **Shortbow.** +5 to hit, 1d6+3 piercing.
- **Clip & Question (Recharge 6).** A grappled or restrained creature makes a DC 12 Wis save or is frightened for 1 round and answers one question truthfully.

**Skills:** Stealth +5, Investigation +5, Insight +5, Intimidation +4
**Equipment:** Steel finger-clipping pliers, dark cloak, boots, a Steen stick to chew, a letter from his father Francisco, badge.
**Hook:** Hates lies and hates being suspected. Has a very personal definition of justice.
