# Harry Sanbi — Level 3 Soul-Touched Wanderer
**People:** "Pure" human with a Bagarisa-touched soul · **Class:** Soul-Touched (homebrew Ranger/Monk hybrid) · **Background:** Isekai Stranger · **Alignment:** Neutral Good · **Rank:** Pearl
**Interface:** none (can't use sigil rings) · **Soul Points:** 3 (+1 extra on each long rest: Deep Soul)

**AC** 13 · **HP** 24 · **Speed** 30 ft
**STR** 12 · **DEX** 14 · **CON** 13 · **INT** 10 · **WIS** 11 · **CHA** 15

### Features & Traits
- **Bagarisa Soul Battery.** Regains 1 extra Soul Point per long rest (stacks with Deep Soul).
- **Confused Memories.** Disadvantage on History checks about Vraki.
- **Claustrophobic.** In tight spaces, DC 10 Wis save or frightened for 1 round.
- **Adaptable Heart.** Advantage on one death save per long rest.
- **Curious Cook.** During a short rest, cooks a meal that grants each eater 1d4+1 temporary HP.

### Actions
- **Unarmed Strike.** +4 to hit, 1d6+2 bludgeoning.
- **Improvised Weapon.** +4 to hit, 1d8+2.
- **Heart Pulse (Recharge 6, or spend 1 SP).** 10-ft cone, 2d6 thunder, DC 11 Con save for half.

### Soul-Energy Abilities (spend SP)
- **Soul Flicker (1 SP).** Bonus action: +10 ft movement this turn.
- **Soul Push (1 SP).** Shove a creature 5 ft without a check.

**Skills:** Persuasion +4, Insight +3, Survival +2, Perception +2
**Equipment:** Traveler's clothes, cooking kit, simple blade, notebook of fragmented memories, badge, 20 pearls.
**Hook:** Nobody believes where he says he's from. Sometimes he isn't sure either.
