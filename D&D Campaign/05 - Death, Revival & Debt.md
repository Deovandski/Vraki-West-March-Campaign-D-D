# Death, Revival & Debt

In Vraki, death is usually a **financial** problem. That's the point.

## Dying
Use normal 5e death saving throws. When you die, your soul stays tied to your body while the body lasts. The dead dream of their strongest memories.

## The revival window
A body can be revived as long as its soul hasn't detached:
- **Revival window = 3 days + 1 day per character level.**
- Keeping the body cold (ice spells, snow, an ice preserver) **doubles** the window. Burned, dissolved or eaten bodies can't be revived.
- After the window closes, the soul dissolves: the character is **Soul Dead** — gone for good.

## Field revival
Within a minute of death: a **black honey revival pot** or *revivify* (the Body RESTORE + Storm CREATE ring pair, Tier III).
Later, within the window: carry the body to any **embassy**. (Ice it. Seriously.)

## Embassy revival
Any embassy revives an adventurer whose body arrives within the revival window. It always works — unless the adventurer has a Walking Heart, is Hash-mutated, or the DM has a very good story reason.
- The adventurer wakes with 1 HP and **one level of exhaustion**.
- They now owe **Revival Debt** to Forway.

### Revival debt
- **Debt = 100 pearls × character level** (a level 3 adventurer owes 300 pearls; a level 10, 1,000).
- Until it's paid, **50% of every quest reward** goes to Forway. You can also pay it off with balls any time.
- **Die again while you still owe** and Forway **drafts you into the Forway Guard**: the character retires as an NPC guard (a great recurring NPC), unless the party pays the full remaining debt plus the new debt on the spot.
- Fleeing your debt makes you a **wanted** criminal (bounty quests appear with your face on them).
- Many adventurers — and the Fallen Tree bandits — call this slavery. They're not entirely wrong.

## Quest deadlines and search parties
Every quest contract has a **deadline** (see Quest Board). If the party isn't back at an embassy by then:
1. The embassy sends a **search party**. Roll 1d6: that's how many days until they reach the last known location.
2. If they find bodies within the revival window, those adventurers are revived with debt — and the **quest credit and reward go to the search party**.
3. Living adventurers who are found get escorted home and owe the search party's fee: **1 amethyst per rank tier** (Pearl 1, Amethyst 2, Aquamarine 3, Sapphire 4) each.
4. Bodies not found in time: **Presumed Soul Dead**. Forway keeps the badge; belongings go to the character's top guild (or to charity after a year).

This is also the West March "get home" rule: a session that ends with the party in the wild means the deadline keeps running until the next session with those characters.

## Walking Hearts and soul death
- A character who gets a **Walking Heart** (see Dangerous Places) becomes undead and **can't be revived** by normal means. Forway law treats them as soul dead.
- A soul fully drained to nothing by a soul-eating creature (Sucker Lady, Walking Flesh) is gone, unless the DM offers a quest to get it back.
